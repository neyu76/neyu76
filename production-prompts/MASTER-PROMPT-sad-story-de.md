# MASTER PROMPT V1.0 — SAD-STORY DRAMA (PHOTOREAL · DEUTSCH) — SEEDANCE + KLING + VEO OMNI
## VERTICAL 9:16 CINEMATIC SHORT — 10s CLIPS · 2-TRACK AUDIO · 140-150 WPM · BILINGUAL CAPCUT HANDOFF

(V1.0 SAD-STORY architecture for the **@heartwarming_stories4** style — **photoreal human tearjerker sagas** in **German**: abandoned/sold/orphaned children and cast-out elders, betrayal → endurance → rescue → cliffhanger. Every clip renders as a uniform **10-second** motion clip in **9:16 vertical** for TikTok / Reels / Shorts.

**CORE FEATURE 1 — PHOTOREAL, NOT ANIMATED.** Real human actors generated by AI. Cinematic European film look, 35mm feel, shallow DOF, desaturated cool grade. NEVER anthropomorphic, NEVER Pixar/Illumination.

**CORE FEATURE 2 — TWO-TRACK AUDIO (the defining architecture).** This genre is NOT per-scene character dialogue. It is:
- **SCENE 1 = THE HOOK FILM (0:00-0:10):** an acted scene with **real lip-synced German dialogue + native audio** (Veo Omni). 1-3 cruel, short lines. The cruel act is already happening.
- **SCENE 2 → N = THE STORY (BODY):** **silent clips** (Seedance/Kling) carried by a **single German NARRATOR** voiceover (ElevenLabs) + SFX + music. Characters in the body do **not** lip-sync dialogue — they react in silence.

**CORE FEATURE 3 — TEXT-WITH-REFERENCE SYNTAX (MAX 15 ASSETS).** Asset handles embedded as exact `@Handle`. Total unique assets NEVER exceed 15.

**CORE FEATURE 4 — WPM SCENE-DIVISION.** Narration paced at **140-150 WPM**; emotional-peak beats may drop to ~30 WPM or full silence — SFX + music carry them.

**CORE FEATURE 5 — TRIPLE MOTION ENGINES, SPLIT BY TRACK.** Phase 2 = Seedance (silent, all scenes, pair with TTS narration). Phase 3 = KLING multi-shot (silent, all scenes). Phase 4 = VEO OMNI (native audio + lip-sync) for the **HOOK scene(s) ONLY**. Body scenes are silent by design.

**CORE FEATURE 6 — CAPCUT HANDOFF.** Phase 5 = German TikTok title package + the assembled **narrator script** + bilingual (DE/VI) scene-by-scene review.

**V1.0 LIP-SYNC & VOICE LOCK:** The HOOK scene hard-locks one speaker per shot, explicit per-line voice gender/age/pitch, and listeners mouth-closed — to kill the two Veo failure modes: (1) the wrong character lip-syncing, and (2) a male character rendering with a female voice. The BODY uses ONE fixed narrator; characters never lip-sync speech.)

**Story logic (Studio Bible, human-mapped → 5-Act):**
- ACT 1 HOOK SHOCK (LOSS+INJUSTICE) — the cruel act is already happening (mother sells the child / father throws the backpack into the rain). Plant the **hero prop** + the **label** ("ein Fehler").

**Visual logic:**
- **Photoreal, cinematic, European film look.** 9:16 vertical; faces upper-middle third, **lower third clear** for captions burned later in CapCut (renders contain NO on-screen text).
- **Grade by emotion:** abandonment/danger = **cool blue-grey, low-key**; memory/love/rescue = **warm amber**; cliffhanger = cool with **one warm point**. The warm zone is the place the victim is NOT allowed to enter (the lit window seen from the rainy street).
- **Pathetic fallacy:** stage abandonment under snow / rain / grey sky.
- **Camera:** close-ups dominate; **low-angle / direct eye-line on the child** = a plea to camera; slow push-ins on emotional peaks; hard-cut energy; cross-cut victim-suffering vs. betrayer-comfort.
- **Rich-poor contrast** legible in one glance (designer coat vs. broken zipper).
