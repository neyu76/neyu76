## LAYER 1 — SHOOTING SCRIPT

**SCENE 1 — Dawn in the bakery | GRADE: warm amber (HOOK)**
- SHOT 1 (CU, 2.5s): Hazel on a flour-dusted stool, oversized eyes reflecting an unfinished birthday cake.
  - **Hazel:** "Is it Mom's cake, Dad?"
- SHOT 2 (CU, 2.5s): Owen's spectacled face, soft smile, piping bag in his wings.
  - **Owen:** "Almost ready, little one."
- SHOT 3 (two-shot, 3s): Hazel leans her head on his wing.
  - **Hazel:** "You always say that."
  - **Owen:** "And I always mean it."

**SCENE 2 — The old oven | GRADE: warm amber, soft grief**
- SHOT 1 (insert, 2s): A soot-darkened brick oven; a faded photo of a barn owl (Mabel) taped to its side.
  - **Owen (quiet):** "Your mom built this oven with her own wings."
- SHOT 2 (low MS, 2.5s): Hazel pats the warm bricks.
  - **Hazel:** "It's the best oven in the whole world."

**SCENE 3 — The bell jingles | GRADE: cold creeps in**
- SHOT 1 (door, 2s): Reynard enters in a sharp suit; Margo trails behind, avoiding eyes.
  - **Reynard:** "So this is the little bakery everyone loves."
- SHOT 2 (low-angle on Reynard, 2.5s): He drags a claw along a dusty shelf.
  - **Reynard:** "Charming. Crumbling, but charming."

**SCENE 4 — The offer | GRADE: cold blue**
- SHOT 1 (OTS Reynard, 3s): He slides a fat envelope across the counter.
  - **Reynard:** "Sell me the recipe. Name your price."
- SHOT 2 (CU Owen, 3s): He gently pushes it back, glancing at Mabel's photo.
  - **Owen:** "It's not for sale. It was my wife's."
  - **Reynard:** "Everything has a price, baker."

**SCENE 5 — The whisper | GRADE: cold, low-key (CROSS-CUT setup)**
- SHOT 1 (tight 2-shot, 3s): Reynard murmurs to Margo by the door; she pockets a second envelope.
  - **Reynard (low):** "You signed your half. That's all I need."
- SHOT 2 (CU Margo, 2.5s): She glances at Owen, then away, ashamed.
  - **Margo (whisper):** "He doesn't know yet."

**SCENE 6 — Later that night | GRADE: warm amber (CROSS-CUT payoff — dramatic irony)**
- SHOT 1 (2-shot, 3s): Owen smooths icing; Hazel hugs his side. We know what he doesn't.
  - **Hazel:** "Will we always have the bakery, Dad?"
- SHOT 2 (CU Owen, 2.5s): A tender, doomed promise.
  - **Owen:** "Always. It's your mom's, and yours."

**SCENE 7 — Morning. The deed | GRADE: cold, hard shadows**
- SHOT 1 (wide, 2s): Reynard in the doorway with a legal document; the cake half-iced behind Owen.
  - **Reynard:** "New ownership, baker. Your partner sold."
- SHOT 2 (push-in Reynard, 3s): He taps the page.
  - **Reynard:** "I own the building, the name, the recipe."
  - **Owen:** "What? Margo, no—"

**SCENE 8 — Margo leaves | GRADE: cold, rain on the window**
- SHOT 1 (CU Margo, 2.5s): She can't meet his eyes, stepping toward Reynard's car.
  - **Margo:** "I'm sorry, Owen. I needed the money."
- SHOT 2 (low-angle Reynard, 2.5s): He smirks down at Owen — THE insult.
  - **Reynard:** "You're crumbs, Owen. Stale, forgotten crumbs."

**SCENE 9 — The dismissal | GRADE: cold → desaturated (PAYLOAD planted)**
- SHOT 1 (Reynard gestures, 2.5s): He waves a dismissive paw at the old brick oven.
  - **Reynard:** "Rip out that filthy old oven. It's worthless."
- SHOT 2 (CU Owen's wing on the bricks, 3s): He touches Mabel's photo.
  - **Owen (whisper):** "She built this with her own wings."

**SCENE 10 — The doorstep | GRADE: cold blue, single warm rim light (CLIFFHANGER)**
- SHOT 1 (MS, 2.5s): Hazel in her yellow scarf, holding the unfinished cake, watching the car pull away.
  - **Hazel:** "He took Mom's bakery."
- SHOT 2 (slow push to CU, 3.5s): Her soft eyes harden into something colder. HOLD the final frame ~1.5s.
  - **Hazel (quiet):** "I'm going to get it back, Dad. Every crumb."
