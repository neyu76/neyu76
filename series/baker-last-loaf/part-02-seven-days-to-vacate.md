# The Baker's Last Loaf — PART 2: "Seven Days to Vacate"

**Beat:** INJUSTICE · **Runtime:** ~95s · **Dialogue words:** ~150 · **Ending:** cliffhanger (the address reveal)
**Cast in part:** Owen, Hazel, Reynard, Foreman (gruff boar, one line) · **Promise object:** birthday cake (boxed, abandoned)
**TOPIC (for master prompt):** Day after losing the bakery, a fox mogul gives the owl baker seven days to vacate, seizes the late wife's recipe book and rebrands the shop as his franchise; broke and homeless in a cramped room, the baker takes a cash demolition job — and is handed the address of his own bakery.

---

## LAYER 1 — SHOOTING SCRIPT

**SCENE 1 — The notice | GRADE: cold (HOOK)**
- SHOT 1 (extreme CU, 2s): A paper nailed to the bakery door — "VACATE IN SEVEN DAYS."
- SHOT 2 (low-angle Reynard, 3s): He pats the door, smug.
  - **Reynard:** "Seven days, baker. Then every brick is mine."

**SCENE 2 — The recipe | GRADE: cold blue**
- SHOT 1 (OTS, 3s): Reynard holds out a paw for a worn, handwritten recipe book.
  - **Reynard:** "The recipe book. It's in the deed. Hand it over."
- SHOT 2 (CU Owen, 3s): He clutches it to his chest.
  - **Owen:** "Please. It's all I have left of her."
  - **Reynard:** "Sentiment doesn't sell pastries."

**SCENE 3 — Mom's handwriting | GRADE: cold (CU innocent)**
- SHOT 1 (CU Hazel, 2.5s): She watches her mother's looping handwriting vanish into Reynard's coat.
  - **Hazel (small):** "That's Mom's writing."

**SCENE 4 — The rebrand | GRADE: garish cold-white (CROSS-CUT)**
- SHOT 1 (wide, 2s): Workers hoist a glowing sign: "REYNARD'S GOLDEN CRUST." The cozy bakery is gutted into a flashy franchise.
- SHOT 2 (Reynard cuts a ribbon, crowd, 3s):
  - **Reynard:** "Old recipe. New empire. All mine."

**SCENE 5 — The cramped room | GRADE: dim, cold (CROSS-CUT)**
- SHOT 1 (wide, 3s): A tiny rented room above a laundromat. One suitcase. The cake box on the windowsill.
  - **Hazel:** "When are we going home, Dad?"
- SHOT 2 (CU Owen, 3s): He forces a small smile.
  - **Owen:** "We'll make a new home, little one."

**SCENE 6 — Counting coins | GRADE: cold, single bulb**
- SHOT 1 (insert, 2.5s): A few coins on a cracked table; the cake box, still unopened.
  - **Owen (whisper):** "Almost ready... almost."

**SCENE 7 — The window | GRADE: cold neon (CROSS-CUT)**
- SHOT 1 (CU Hazel at glass, 3s): She presses to the franchise window, watching strangers eat her mom's cake under the fox's logo.
  - **Hazel (quiet):** "He's selling Mom."

**SCENE 8 — Desperation | GRADE: grey overcast**
- SHOT 1 (insert, 2s): A flyer: "DEMOLITION CREW — CASH DAILY."
- SHOT 2 (CU Owen, 2.5s): He pockets it, jaw set.
  - **Owen:** "I can swing a hammer. I'll take it."

**SCENE 9 — The address | GRADE: cold, hard shadow (CLIFFHANGER)**
- SHOT 1 (OTS Foreman, 3s): A gruff boar foreman slaps a card into Owen's wing.
  - **Foreman:** "Big job tomorrow. Here's the address."
- SHOT 2 (extreme CU on the card, then Owen's frozen face, 4s): He reads it. His amber eyes go hollow. HOLD ~1.5s.
  - **Owen (barely a breath):** "...That's my bakery."
