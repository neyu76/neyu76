# The Baker's Last Loaf — PART 3: "Tear It Down"

**Beat:** ENDURANCE · **Runtime:** ~95s · **Dialogue words:** ~120 (intentionally low — let the labor + score breathe) · **Ending:** cliffhanger (Hazel's plan begins)
**Cast in part:** Owen, Hazel, Reynard, Foreman · **Promise object:** —  · **Payload:** the old oven is hauled to the scrapyard (sets up Part 6)
**TOPIC (for master prompt):** Broke, the owl baker shows up for a cash demolition job and finds the site is his own bakery; he is forced to gut it and rip out his late wife's brick oven while the fox mogul mocks him — as his daughter, watching, hardens from grief into a plan.

## LAYER 1 — SHOOTING SCRIPT

**SCENE 1 — The orange vest | GRADE: dusty overcast (HOOK)**
- SHOT 1 (CU, 2.5s): Owen, in a too-big orange demolition vest, stares up at his own gutted bakery, sledgehammer in his wings.
- SHOT 2 (OTS Foreman, 3s):
  - **Foreman:** "You're the demo guy? Start inside. All of it comes out today."
  - **Owen (hollow):** "My wife built this place."

**SCENE 2 — Breaking the home | GRADE: desaturated (near-silent, ~no dialogue)**
- SHOT 1 (insert, 2.5s): His crowbar pries off the counter where Hazel used to sit.
- SHOT 2 (slow CU, 3s): Dust falls through the light. His jaw trembles. Somber piano, a heartbeat. No words.

**SCENE 3 — Reynard's taunt | GRADE: cold**
- SHOT 1 (low-angle Reynard, 3s): He strolls in, sipping coffee, gold tie clip gleaming.
  - **Reynard:** "Orange suits you, baker."
  - **Reynard:** "You're better at breaking things than baking them."

**SCENE 4 — Not the oven | GRADE: cold blue**
- SHOT 1 (Reynard points, 2.5s): He flicks a claw at the old brick oven, Mabel's photo still taped to it.
  - **Reynard:** "And that ugly oven. Out. It's worthless junk."
- SHOT 2 (CU Owen, 2.5s):
  - **Owen:** "Not the oven. Please."

**SCENE 5 — His own wings | GRADE: desaturated, single shaft of light (GUT-PUNCH, near-silent)**
- SHOT 1 (CU wings on the crowbar, 3s): They shake. He sets the bar against the bricks his wife laid.
- SHOT 2 (CU Owen, whispering to the photo, 3s):
  - **Owen (whisper):** "Forgive me, Mabel."
- SHOT 3 (cross-cut, CU Hazel, 2.5s): Across the street, Hazel watches, frozen, eyes shining.

**SCENE 6 — Hauled away | GRADE: cold, grey rain**
- SHOT 1 (wide, 3s): The oven is dragged onto a scrap truck, Mabel's photo fluttering on its side.
  - **Reynard (offscreen):** "Haul it to the scrapyard with the rest of the trash."

**SCENE 7 — The photo | GRADE: cold, one warm rim light (CU innocent)**
- SHOT 1 (insert, 2s): Mabel's faded photo flutters loose, lands in a puddle.
- SHOT 2 (CU Hazel, 3s): She picks it up, presses it to her chest.
  - **Hazel (quiet):** "I'll get it all back."

**SCENE 8 — Where the oven stood | GRADE: garish cold-white**
- SHOT 1 (Reynard gestures, 3s): Workers wheel in a gaudy golden display case where the oven was.
  - **Reynard:** "A golden case. Right where that junk used to be."

**SCENE 9 — The plan | GRADE: cold blue (CLIFFHANGER)**
- SHOT 1 (CU Hazel, 3s): From the shadows she watches Reynard slip into a back room with a twitchy rat in an oily raincoat.
- SHOT 2 (slow push to CU, 3.5s): Her soft eyes narrow. HOLD ~1.5s.
  - **Hazel (quiet):** "He's hiding something. And I'm going to find it."
