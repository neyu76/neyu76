# SERIES BIBLE — "The Baker's Last Loaf"

Source topic: #1 from `series-templates/topic-bank-30.md`.
Built with the `script-animal-drama` skill + the Studio Bible. 7-part serialized
short-form saga (~90-100s per part), 9:16 vertical, hard-cut animal drama.

---

## Logline
A widowed owl baker, who keeps his late wife alive through her secret cake
recipe, is betrayed by his own partner and stripped of his bakery by a fox
franchise mogul — who sneers at the one thing hiding a fortune: her old brick oven.

## Voices (TTS assignment)
- Owen — warm, weary. · Hazel — small, earnest. · Reynard — smooth, smug.
- Margo — cold, guilty. · Bruin — flat, calm authority. · Skiv — nasal, nervous.

## Through-line
- **Promise object:** Hazel's **birthday cake** (Mabel's secret recipe). States: started (P1) → left unfinished/abandoned (P2-P5) → finished at last (P7).
- **Catchphrase:** **"Almost ready, little one."** — said while baking (P1), and fulfilled in the finale (P7).
- **Insult to reverse:** **"You're crumbs, Owen. Stale, forgotten crumbs."** (P1) → reversed silently when Owen pulls up to buy back everything (P6).
- **Poetic-justice payload:** gold coins Mabel sealed inside the **old brick oven**. Reynard calls it "worthless" and orders it ripped out (P1/P3); it's hauled to a scrapyard; Owen buys the "worthless" oven back and finds the gold (P6). Reynard learns the truth in prison (P7).

## Visual & audio signature
- Grade: villain/scheming = cold blue-gray; family/love = warm amber; rebirth = glowing gold; karma = blue-red flash. Flip cold→gold at P6.
- Signature insert: the old brick oven + Mabel's faded photo.
- Signature sound: the scrape of a piping bag / the warm hum of the oven (callback cue); silence + heartbeat for the gut-punches.
