## Logline
A widowed ox farmer, evicted by an arrogant tiger landlord and sold out by his sworn
brother, endures one last brutal season to feed the orphaned lamb he raised — until
the "worthless" field he refuses to leave gives up the hidden deed that owns the
tyrant's mansion.

## Worlds

- **@HillEstate** — Rajan's white-pillared mansion above the valley. Grade: glowing gold → blue-red flash (karma).

## Through-line
- **Promise object:** **@FathersPlow** — Bo's hand-worn wooden plow, handle carved with Bin's name. States: appears/gripped (P1) → mended through hardship (P2-P3) → handle splits to reveal the tin (P3) → carries the proof to court (P4-P6) → stands on the reclaimed gold hill (P7).
- **Catchphrase:** **"This field remembers."** — said at the grave (P1), echoed in Bin's vow (P1), fulfilled in the finale (P7).
- **Insult to reverse:** **"Dirt-farmers don't own dirt."** (P1) → reversed silently in P7 when Bo holds the deed to the very hill the mansion stands on.
- **Poetic-justice payload:** **@DeedTin** — the original land-deed sealed in a tin inside the plow's hollow handle, naming Bo as true owner of the estate's ground. Seeded P1 (plow reads sacred/heavy, no metal shown) → handle splits, metal glints P3 → Vera confirms the seal P4 → detonated P7 (the mansion sits on Bo's titled land; Rajan evicted from his own gate).
