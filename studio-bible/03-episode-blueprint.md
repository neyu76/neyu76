# 03 — Episode Blueprint

How to structure a single part (one video) so it hooks in second one, runs
~60-75s, and ends on a cliffhanger. Copy the template at the bottom for each part.

---

## The hard numbers (TikTok-native)

- **Runtime:** 55-80 seconds per part. Sweet spot ~65s.
- **Shots:** 18-30 shots per part. Each shot **1.5-3s**.
- **Dialogue:** 110-170 words per part (the source series sits here). The
  "reward" episode can go very low (~40 words) and lean on visuals + music.
- **Scenes:** 3-4 scenes per part. Each scene = 3-7 shots.
- **Hook:** the core line or the injustice must land in the **first 1-2 seconds**. No logos, no slow establishing shots.

---

## The per-part skeleton (4 beats inside one video)

1. **HOOK (0-2s):** open on the strongest image or line of the part. Often a
   line of contempt ("You're a loser") or a shock (the foreclosure paper, the
   empty bed). Start *in* the conflict.
2. **ESCALATE (2-40s):** 2-3 scenes that raise the stakes. Use cross-cutting
   between hero and villain to keep rhythm. Each scene tops the previous.
3. **GUT-PUNCH / TURN (40-60s):** the emotional peak of the part — the betrayal
   lands, the innocent reacts, or the trap is sprung.
4. **CLIFFHANGER (last 3-8s):** end on an unanswered question or a vow. Hold on a
   face. Optional on-screen text: "Part X" / "to be continued".

---

## Cliffhanger menu (rotate these so endings don't feel samey)

- **The vow:** innocent says "I'm gonna take everything you have." (P3)
- **The trap armed:** the call is made / the plan is set, outcome unknown. (P4)
- **The reveal pending:** authority shows up with evidence, cut before the reaction. (P5)
- **The roles cross:** hero and villain lock eyes, no words. (P6)
- **The name:** villain learns who beat them, says the hero's name. (P7 / finale)
- **The empty space:** something/someone is gone (empty bed, torn-down swing).

---

## Opening-line bank (first 2 seconds)

Use a *line* or a *shocking visual* as the cold open. Examples to adapt:
- "You're a loser. You always were."
- "You have 7 days to vacate."
- "Grab a sledge. The whole thing comes down today."
- "We found a bag with your prints all over it."
- "Some guy bought it. Cash, same day."

---

## On-screen text & captions

- **Burned-in captions** for every line (most viewers watch muted). Big, bold, high-contrast, centered-lower third.
- **Part label** top corner ("PART 3"), and a 1-3 word episode title that doubles as a hook ("THE DEMOLITION").
- Keep captions to ~4-6 words on screen at a time, synced to the voiceover.

```
  Shot 1 — [image] — LINE: "[strongest line]"

```

---

## Quick QA before you call a part "done"

- [ ] Does the injustice/hook land in the first 2 seconds?
- [ ] Is there at least one close-up of the innocent's face?
- [ ] Did I cross-cut hero vs. villain at least once?
- [ ] Did I touch a recurring motif (catchphrase / promise object)?
- [ ] Does it end on a cliffhanger, not a resolution?
- [ ] Are all lines burned in as captions?
- [ ] Is every shot 1.5-3s (no shot lingers)?
