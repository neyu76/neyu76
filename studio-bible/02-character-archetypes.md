# 02 — Character Archetypes & Animal Casting

This is the part that lets you **scale beyond dog/cat/wolf to any animal.**

The trick the source series uses: it doesn't pick animals at random. It exploits
**pre-loaded cultural stereotypes** so the audience instantly "gets" each
character with zero exposition. A wolf in a suit *reads* as a predator-tycoon in
one frame. Use that shortcut every time.

---

## The 6 core roles (every series needs these)

| Role | Function | Visual signature | Source-series example |
|------|----------|------------------|------------------------|
| **THE HERO** | Underestimated, honest, hardworking; loses everything, earns it back | Work clothes, worn but clean, warm colors | Dog (carpenter) |
| **THE TYRANT** | Rich, arrogant, powerful villain who takes from the hero | Sharp suit, luxury car, cold colors | Wolf |
| **THE BETRAYER** | Trusted insider who defects to the Tyrant for money/status | Glam, jewelry, status symbols | Cat (mother) |
| **THE INNOCENT** | The emotional anchor; suffers, then drives the righteous turn | Big eyes, bright single-color outfit | Pup (child) |
| **THE JUSTICE** | Calm authority who delivers the karma (cop/lawyer/judge) | Uniform, badge, glasses, neutral palette | Bear (detective) |
| **THE HENCHMAN** | The Tyrant's accomplice; often leaks the fatal secret | Shifty, dark coat, nervous | Snake / Hyena / Fox |

Optional supporting roles: **The Mentor/Helper** (gives the hero a break — banker
Moose, kind foreman), **The Rival** (a second victim or competitor), **The
Bystander Chorus** (neighbors who witness the downfall).

## Casting table — map ANY animal to a role by its stereotype

Pick the animal whose "vibe" the audience already shares. You almost never need
to explain personality if the casting is right.

### HERO candidates (sympathetic, hardworking, loyal, underestimated)
| Animal | Pre-loaded read | Great hero job |
|--------|-----------------|----------------|
| Dog | loyal, blue-collar, devoted father | carpenter, mechanic |
| Beaver | tireless builder, craftsman | construction, engineer |
| Ox / Buffalo | strong, patient, silent endurer | farmer, laborer |
| Donkey | humble, mocked, dependable | delivery, farmhand |
| Mouse / Hamster | small, overlooked, scrappy | clerk, repair |
| Elephant | gentle giant, great memory, dignified | dad who never forgets a slight |
| Bear (gentle variant) | warm, protective provider | baker, fisherman |
| Tortoise | slow, patient, wins long game | shopkeeper, gardener |
| Sheep | gentle, underestimated single parent | tailor, shepherd |
| Pig | hardworking, mocked as "dirty/poor" but secretly clever | farmer, cook |

### TYRANT candidates (powerful, arrogant, predatory, "alpha")
| Animal | Pre-loaded read | Great villain role |
|--------|-----------------|--------------------|
| Wolf | apex predator, sigma/alpha, cunning | tycoon, crime boss |
| Lion | "king", entitled, dominant | CEO, mob boss, landlord |
| Tiger | ruthless, solitary power | corrupt developer |
| Crocodile / Gator | cold-blooded, no mercy | loan shark, slumlord |
| Shark | predatory finance | banker, investor villain |
| Gorilla | brute force intimidation | enforcer-boss |
| Eagle / Hawk | looks down on everyone, swoops in | corporate raider |
| Rhino | unstoppable, dumb-strong arrogance | bully developer |
| Boar | aggressive, greedy | land grabber |

### BETRAYER candidates (vain, materialistic, defects for status)
| Animal | Pre-loaded read |
|--------|-----------------|
| Cat | aloof, self-interested, glam |
| Peacock | vain, obsessed with appearances |
| Fox | sly, opportunistic, two-faced |
| Snake | treacherous, "snake in the grass" |
| Magpie / Crow | drawn to shiny things, steals |
| Flamingo | showy, social-climbing |
| Weasel | "weaselly", slippery |

### INNOCENT candidates (the anchor — usually the hero's child or ward)
| Animal | Pre-loaded read |
|--------|-----------------|
| Puppy / Kitten / any "-cub/-pup/calf/chick" of the hero's species | pure, vulnerable |
| Duckling | tiny, cute, brave |
| Lamb | gentle, "innocent lamb" |
| Bunny | soft, big-eyed, sympathetic |
| Fawn | fragile, wide-eyed |
| Chick | small, helpless, plucky |

> Tip: make the INNOCENT a **baby of the HERO's species** for instant family
> bonding (Dog -> Pup). Or contrast species to imply adoption/blended family.

### JUSTICE candidates (authority, calm, incorruptible)
| Animal | Pre-loaded read | Role |
|--------|-----------------|------|
| Bear | imposing but fair | detective |
| Owl | wise, sees in the dark | judge, lawyer |
| Elephant | never forgets, dignified | judge |
| German-Shepherd / K9 Dog | police instinct | cop |
| Lion (good variant) | authoritative justice | chief / judge |
| Bull / Ox (in uniform) | solid, immovable | beat cop |

### HENCHMAN candidates (accomplice, leaks the secret, expendable)
| Animal | Pre-loaded read |
|--------|-----------------|
| Snake | safecracker, schemer |
| Hyena | laughing sidekick, betrays for self |
| Fox | fixer, lawyer, money mover |
| Rat | informant, sewer-level crook |
| Jackal | scavenger, follows the powerful |
| Vulture | waits to pick the bones (repo man, undertaker) |

## Casting rules (so it never feels arbitrary)

1. **Predator vs. prey = power vs. virtue.** Default: Tyrant is a predator, Hero
   is a builder/prey/working animal. The size/power mismatch *is* the injustice.
2. **Never reuse one animal for two roles in the same series.** One wolf, one cat, etc.
3. **The Betrayer should be close to the Hero** (spouse, partner, sibling, best
   friend) — betrayal only hurts if there was trust.
4. **The Justice animal should out-rank the Tyrant in dignity**, not in flash. A
   plain-suited Bear detective beats a flashy Wolf — that contrast sells karma.
5. **Match jobs to nature.** Beaver builds, Shark does finance, Owl judges,
   Magpie steals. The audience nods before you explain anything.
6. **Keep silhouettes distinct** so thumbnails read instantly at small size.

## Ready-to-shoot cast presets (mix & match per series)

Each preset is a complete 6-role cast you can drop into the Story Engine.

- **Preset A — "Builder's Betrayal"**
  Hero: **Beaver** (builder) · Tyrant: **Lion** (developer) · Betrayer: **Peacock** (wife) ·
  Innocent: **Kit (baby beaver)** · Justice: **Owl** (judge) · Henchman: **Hyena**.
- **Preset B — "The Farmer's Land"**
  Hero: **Ox** (farmer) · Tyrant: **Crocodile** (loan shark) · Betrayer: **Fox** (brother-in-law) ·
  Innocent: **Calf** · Justice: **Bear** (sheriff) · Henchman: **Rat**.
- **Preset C — "Feathers & Greed"**
  Hero: **Sparrow/Pigeon** (street musician) · Tyrant: **Eagle** (label boss) · Betrayer: **Flamingo** (manager) ·
  Innocent: **Chick** · Justice: **Elephant** (judge) · Henchman: **Vulture**.
- **Preset D — "The Baker's Recipe"**
  Hero: **Bear** (baker) · Tyrant: **Tiger** (franchise tycoon) · Betrayer: **Cat** (partner) ·
  Innocent: **Bunny** (apprentice/ward) · Justice: **Owl** (inspector/judge) · Henchman: **Weasel**.
- **Preset E — "Underwater Empire"**
  Hero: **Sea Turtle** (boat repair) · Tyrant: **Shark** (resort mogul) · Betrayer: **Dolphin** (partner) ·
  Innocent: **Seal pup** · Justice: **Orca** (coast guard) · Henchman: **Eel**.

> To invent your own: pick a HERO with a craft, a TYRANT predator that can plausibly
> ruin that craft, a BETRAYER close to the hero, an INNOCENT to protect, a JUSTICE
> to land the karma, and a HENCHMAN to leak the secret. Then run the Story Engine.
