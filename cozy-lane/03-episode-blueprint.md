# 03 — Episode Blueprint (Cozy Lane)

How to structure a single cozy episode so it soothes, holds attention with a
*worry* loop (not dread), and ends safe & warm. This lane is **slow on purpose** —
the opposite of the revenge lane's 55–80s hard-cut sprint.

---

## The hard numbers (cozy, dual-format)

| Spec | Long-form (primary) | Shorts (funnel) |
|------|---------------------|-----------------|
| Aspect | **16:9** 1920×1080 | **9:16** 1080×1920 |
| Runtime | **6–10 min** (sweet spot ~8) | **20–45s** |
| Shot length | **4–8s** (let it breathe) | 2–4s |
| Shots | ~70–110 | 8–15 |
| Dialogue | **none** — non-verbal vocalizations only | none |
| Scenes | 6–9 (one per arc beat + connective tissue) | 1 tender/wonder moment |
| Hook | a gentle "wrongness" or the **guest** within **0–8s** | the single most emotive frame first |
| Transitions | hard cuts **+ occasional soft dissolves** on time-passing | hard cuts |

> Why slow: the product is *reassurance*. Shots that breathe let the viewer
> co-regulate (it's why this works for sleep). If a shot has no new *feeling* or
> *information*, it's too long — but "a held, beautiful, calm image" **is**
> information in this lane.

---

## The episode skeleton (the 6-beat Comfort Arc in one video)

Map each scene to a beat from `01-comfort-engine.md`. Times are for an ~8-min episode.

1. **CALM (0:00–1:00):** establish the cozy normal — the duo's bond, the world,
   the **signature prop at rest/glowing**, and the **silent ritual**. Plant the
   **kindness-echo** here (a tiny throwaway kindness to a Chorus critter).
   *Hook lives in the last beat of CALM: the first "something's wrong."*
2. **RIPPLE (1:00–2:15):** the gentle sadness arrives — the **Guest-in-need**
   appears (hurt/lost/displaced) or nature is in trouble. The prop dims a touch.
   Pose the **worry loop**: *will they be okay?*
3. **REACH (2:15–3:45):** the duo chooses to help and sets out; a beat of
   **wonder/discovery** (a beautiful place, a glimpse of magic). First obstacle appears.
4. **EFFORT & SETBACK (3:45–5:30):** they try — and **fail once**. The lowest
   point; it looks lost; the prop dims most here. *(This is the beat copycats skip
   — never skip it.)*
5. **TENDERNESS (5:30–6:45):** the turn. A quiet act of care lands; the
   **kindness-echo detonates** (the critter you helped in CALM now helps them).
   The emotional peak — the "gut-hug."
6. **RESTORATION (6:45–8:00):** the hurt heals, the guest **belongs**, the **prop
   glows brightest**, the duo repeat their **silent ritual**. Fade to a peaceful,
   golden close. Optional 10–15s **end-card nature fact**.

## Hook menu (the first 0–8s — replace "shock" with "tender pull")

The revenge lane opens on contempt or shock. The cozy lane opens on a **soft pull**:

- **The tender discovery:** the duo finds the guest (a trembling shape in the leaves).
- **The beautiful wrongness:** a gorgeous shot where *one thing is sad* (a glowing forest with one grey, felled patch).
- **The small cry:** a faint sound off-screen; Wonder's ears perk; we follow.
- **The prop reacts first:** the lantern flickers/dims before we see why — curiosity.
- **The face:** open on the Wonder's worried/awed expression (the thumbnail frame), then reveal what they see.

> The thumbnail already previewed the guest — the hook **confirms** that curiosity
> within seconds (the competitor's strongest retention move). Don't bury it.

---

## Worry-loop menu (rotate so episodes don't feel samey — none use dread)

- **Will they reach it in time?** (before the cold night / the storm / dark)
- **Will the little one make it?** (the injured/sick guest)
- **Will they find the way home?** (the lost guest)
- **Will it grow back?** (the dying tree/sapling)
- **Will the duo be okay?** (a gentle peril to the *duo* — softest, use sparingly)
- **The prop's light:** the dimming lantern as a visible countdown of hope.

Every loop **must** resolve warm by RESTORATION. Never end an episode on an open worry.

---

## On-screen text & the no-dialogue rule

- **No spoken language, ever.** Story is carried by **image + sound + the prop**
  (see `05-sound-and-wordless-storytelling.md`).
- **Minimal text:** an optional **title card** (short, evocative — "THE BROKEN
  FOREST") and an optional **end-card fact** (1 sentence, localizable; or icon-only
  to stay 100% language-free).
- **Captions:** none needed (no dialogue) — a key reason this localizes for free.
- **Non-verbal vocalizations only** (soft chirps, gasps, hums) — emotion without language.

## Bedtime / long-form compilation variant (the sleep moat)

A monthly **45–60 min** video, built for auto-play through the night:

- **Stitch 3–4 finished episodes** of the *same calm energy* (avoid the highest-peril ones).
- **Ambient bridges (30–60s)** between episodes: a slow nature scene + continuous soft score so it never "jolts."
- **Descending energy curve:** order episodes most-engaging → most-soothing so it lulls toward sleep.
- **Loop-friendly ending:** end on a near-silent starlit Hollow Glen scene that could loop back to the top.
- **No mid-roll jolts:** keep music continuous; set ads to respect the calm where possible.
- **Title for search:** "Calm Bedtime Stories for Kids • 1 Hour • No Talking • [Theme]" (see `05` for the formula).

## Quick QA before you call an episode "done"

- [ ] Does the tender hook land in the first 8 seconds?
- [ ] Is the **Guest-in-need** clearly sympathetic within the first minute?
- [ ] Did they **fail once** before succeeding (effort, not magic)?
- [ ] Did the **kindness-echo** get planted in CALM and pay off by TENDERNESS?
- [ ] Does the **signature prop** track the emotion (rest → dim → glow)?
- [ ] Did the **silent ritual** appear at least twice (CALM + RESTORATION)?
- [ ] Does it **end safe & warm** (no open dread)?
- [ ] Zero spoken language; story readable with sound off *and* rewarding with sound on?
- [ ] Are 2–3 **Shorts pulls** marked for the funnel?
- [ ] Pacing breathes (shots ~4–8s); soft dissolves only on time-passing?
