# 02 — Character Archetypes & Cozy Casting

This is the part that lets the cozy lane **scale beyond one duo to a whole gentle
universe** — and, crucially, the part that builds the **merch moat**.

Like the revenge lane (`/studio-bible/02`), we exploit **pre-loaded cultural
reads** so the audience "gets" each character instantly with zero words (which we
don't have anyway). The difference: instead of casting *predator vs prey = power
vs virtue*, we cast **soft, rounded, nurturing reads** — and we design the leads
**plush-first**.

---

## The 5 cozy roles (most episodes need 3–4 of these)

| Role | Function | Visual signature | In our worked example |
|------|----------|------------------|------------------------|
| **THE PROTECTOR** | Calm, steady, capable caregiver; leads the help; the "parent" energy | Sturdy round silhouette, warm earth tones, a small useful prop (satchel/tool) | **Bramble** (bear cub) |
| **THE WONDER** | Childlike, curious, expressive, easily moved; the audience's eyes; tends the **signature prop** | Tiny, oversized eyes, one bright accent, the prop in-hand | **Wisp** (snowy owlet) |
| **THE GUEST-IN-NEED** | The episode's secret protagonist — hurt, lost, frightened, or displaced | Designed for instant sympathy: wide eyes, droop, smallness | rotating (eagle, fawn, mole family…) |
| **THE WORLD / FORCE** | The "conflict": circumstance, never malice — a storm, a flood, a felled grove, a cold night | Impersonal: weather, machines, broken nature (no face, no smirk) | the broken forest / the storm |
| **THE GENTLE FOIL** *(optional)* | Mild, comedic friction or a grumpy-then-softened neighbor; lightens tone | Slightly prickly silhouette, soft underneath | e.g., a grumpy old tortoise |
| **THE GLEN CHORUS** *(optional, recurring)* | Background regulars who make the world feel lived-in & set up the **kindness-echo** | A handful of recurring small critters | the firefly swarm, a beetle, a hedgehog family |

> There is **no Tyrant, no Betrayer, no Henchman.** If you find yourself casting a
> villain, you've drifted into the revenge lane. The opposition here is the
> **World/Force**, and it is always impersonal and survivable.

---

## The signature DUO — design rules (this is the IP and the moat)

The two leads are the brand. They must be **merch-able, instantly readable in
silhouette, and emotionally complementary.** Lock these before episode 1.

### 1. The complementary pair
- **Protector + Wonder.** One is *calm and grounded*, the other is *curious and
  emotive*. The audience projects onto the Wonder and feels safe with the
  Protector. (Roro = protector, Dodo = wonder — the dynamic that works.)
- Give them a **shared silent ritual** (foreheads touch, a double head-pat) — this
  is the wordless "catchphrase" (see `01-comfort-engine`).

### 2. Plush-first silhouettes
- **Round, soft, low-detail-readable.** If it can't be a recognizable plush at
  10 cm, redesign it. Avoid sharp/aggressive shapes.
- **Distinct silhouettes from each other** (e.g., a round bear vs a tiny owlet) so
  thumbnails read at a glance.
- **One signature accent each** (Bramble's grass-cloak, Wisp's amber eyes) — the
  detail that survives onto a sticker.

### 3. The signature prop (the merch keystone)
- The duo **owns one prop** that is the **emotional barometer + the hero product**
  (a firefly **lantern**, a music box, a seed pouch). See `01-comfort-engine`.
- It must be **drawable in one stroke**, glow/change state, and be **sellable as a
  physical object** beside the plush.

### 4. Ownability vs readability (pick your moat strength)
| Approach | Readability | Moat / ownability | Use when |
|----------|:-----------:|:-----------------:|----------|
| **Real animals** (bear + owlet) | ★★★★★ | ★★★ | Fastest to launch; rely on prop + style for moat |
| **Real animals + signature prop/quirk** *(recommended)* | ★★★★★ | ★★★★ | Default: instant read **and** an ownable detail |
| **Original creatures** (invented species) | ★★★ | ★★★★★ | Max defensibility (you own the species) but slower to land |

> Recommended default: **real-animal duo + a signature prop + a locked style.**
> Instant readability *plus* an ownable detail a prompt-clone can't reproduce.

## Casting tables — map ANY animal to a cozy role by its "soft read"

Pick the animal whose gentle vibe the audience already shares; you never explain
personality if the casting is right.

### PROTECTOR candidates (calm, steady, capable, nurturing)
| Animal | Pre-loaded read |
|--------|-----------------|
| Bear cub | warm, sturdy, safe, "big sibling" |
| Capybara | unflappable, serene, the internet's calm icon *(also our LatAm hero → universe synergy)* |
| Tortoise | patient, wise, slow & reassuring |
| Moose / deer (adult) | gentle giant, watchful |
| Golden retriever / dog | loyal, comforting |
| Sheepdog | herder, protector of the small |

### WONDER candidates (childlike, curious, expressive, the audience's eyes)
| Animal | Pre-loaded read |
|--------|-----------------|
| Owlet | huge eyes, curious, easily wowed |
| Duckling | tiny, brave, eager |
| Fox kit | playful, inquisitive |
| Hedgehog | shy-then-delighted, adorable |
| Dormouse / fieldmouse | tiny, soft, plucky |
| Bunny | big-eyed, gentle, expressive |
| Otter pup | playful, joyful |

### GUEST-IN-NEED candidates (instant sympathy; rotate every episode)
| Type | Pre-loaded read | Episode premise it unlocks |
|------|-----------------|----------------------------|
| Injured raptor (eagle/owl) | proud + wounded → vulnerable | rescue & heal, then release |
| Lost fawn / cub | tiny + alone | reunite with family / shelter from storm |
| Displaced family (moles, rabbits) | home destroyed | rebuild / relocate a home (nature pillar) |
| Cold/sick small bird | shivering, fragile | warmth & care through a cold night |
| Trapped creature (net, mud, ice) | panic → relief | patient, clever freeing |
| A dying tree / sapling | nature itself as "guest" | nurse it back (the 24M pillar) |

### WORLD / FORCE candidates (the impersonal conflict)
| Force | Feeling | Keep it… |
|-------|---------|----------|
| A felled / dying grove | grief for nature | hopeful — regrowth is possible |
| A storm / flood / blizzard | peril, urgency | survivable, never deadly on-screen |
| A drought / dry spring | slow worry | solvable with effort + a water source |
| A cold night | smallness | answered with warmth & the lantern |
| Distant machines (off-screen) | unease | impersonal; never a gloating operator |

### GENTLE FOIL candidates (optional comic/soft friction)
| Animal | Pre-loaded read |
|--------|-----------------|
| Grumpy old tortoise/badger | crusty outside, soft inside |
| Fussy squirrel | hoards, then shares |
| Sleepy sloth | always behind, lovable |

## Cozy casting rules (so it never feels arbitrary)

1. **Soft reads only for the leads.** Protector = sturdy/serene; Wonder = tiny/curious. No apex-predator energy on the duo.
2. **The Guest rotates; the duo never changes.** Novelty lives in the guest (subject escalation); comfort lives in the constant duo.
3. **No animal is "evil."** A wolf or hawk may appear, but only as a *frightened* or *misunderstood* guest, never a villain.
4. **The Force has no face.** Conflict is weather, time, injury, or off-screen machines — impersonal and survivable.
5. **Keep silhouettes distinct & round** so thumbnails read at small size and plush translate cleanly.
6. **Match help to nature.** Beavers mend dams, moles dig shelters, fireflies light the dark — the audience nods before you explain.
7. **Plant the Chorus early** — a recurring small critter you're kind to in beat 1 is your **kindness-echo** payoff in beat 4–5.

## Ready-to-shoot DUO presets (drop into the Comfort Engine)

Each preset is a complete lead pair + signature prop you can build a channel on.

- **Preset A — "Bramble & Wisp" (our worked example)**
  Protector: **Bramble** (bear cub, grass-cloak, repair satchel) · Wonder: **Wisp**
  (snowy owlet, amber eyes) · Prop: **firefly lantern** · World: **Hollow Glen**.
- **Preset B — "Coco & Tito"** *(LatAm-synergy)*
  Protector: **Coco** (capybara, serene) · Wonder: **Tito** (tiny marmoset/duckling)
  · Prop: **a clay rain-bell** · World: a riverside cloud-forest. *(Localizes natively to es/pt.)*
- **Preset C — "Pebble & Fern"**
  Protector: **Pebble** (tortoise, mossy shell) · Wonder: **Fern** (fox kit) · Prop:
  **a seed pouch** that sprouts what they plant · World: a meadow-garden.
- **Preset D — "Nori & Bun"**
  Protector: **Nori** (golden retriever) · Wonder: **Bun** (dormouse) · Prop: **a
  patchwork quilt** that gathers patches from each rescued friend · World: a snowy hamlet.
- **Preset E — "Original-IP" (max moat)**
  Two invented soft creatures (think Totoro/Pikmin energy) + a glowing prop. Hardest
  to land, strongest to own. Use once the brand has traction.

> To invent your own: pick a **calm Protector** + a **curious Wonder**, give them a
> **silent ritual** and a **signature prop** (that's also merch), set them in a
> **named cozy world**, and rotate a **Guest-in-need** each episode. Then run the
> Comfort Engine in `01-comfort-engine.md`.
