## 1. Logline
A gentle bear cub, **Bramble**, and a tiny, wonder-struck snowy owlet, **Wisp**,
live in **Hollow Glen** and tend a little **firefly lantern**. Each episode they
find a creature — or the forest itself — hurting, and through patience, cleverness,
and kindness (never magic alone) they heal it so everyone ends safe and warm.

- **Series title:** "Bramble & Wisp: Hollow Glen Tales"

## 4. The through-line
- **Signature prop:** the **firefly lantern** — a round glass lantern with one gentle firefly inside. States: glow (CALM) → dim (RIPPLE) → near-dark (SETBACK) → re-kindle (TENDERNESS) → brightest (RESTORATION). **This is the hero merch product** (plush + a real glowing lantern toy).
- **Silent ritual:** Bramble gently **touches foreheads** with Wisp, then **pats Wisp's head twice**. Means "I've got you." Used in CALM and RESTORATION every episode.
- **Kindness-echo (series-level signature):** the duo are kind to the **firefly swarm / Bo the beetle** early; in the dark low point, the swarm/beetle returns to **light the way** — kindness, repaid.
- **The promise:** every episode ends **safe & warm**. Always.

## 5. The 6-beat Comfort Arc (series default)

| Beat | Function | Grade/light | Lantern |
|------|----------|-------------|---------|
| CALM | Glen morning, the bond, plant a kindness | warm golden | glow |

## 7. Visual & sound notes
- Palette: Glen lives in **warm amber-green golden hour**; dip to **cool blue-grey** only at RIPPLE/SETBACK (the felled grove is desaturated grey), then bloom back to gold; the lantern is always the warm key light.

- Leitmotifs: **Bramble = a warm, low cello phrase** · **Wisp = a light celesta/flute phrase** · **lantern = a soft 3-note chime**.

### EP 6 — THE BROKEN FOREST  (~8 min) — the HERO/nature episode

**CALM (0:00–1:00)** — *warm cello + celesta; birdsong; stream.*
Dawn in Hollow Glen. Bramble re-hangs the **firefly lantern** (steady glow) on a
root; Wisp spirals down, delighted, and they **touch foreheads / double head-pat**.
Wisp offers a berry to **Bo the beetle**, who trundles off happy. *(plant the
kindness-echo.)* Wisp's ears perk — a faint, wrong sound on the wind.
- **HOOK (~0:52):** the birdsong cuts out. Silence. Wisp tilts toward the sound.

**RIPPLE (1:00–2:15)** — *strings thin out; a cool blue creeps in; lantern dims a touch.*
They follow the quiet to the Glen's edge — and stop. A whole stretch of forest is
**felled and grey**, stumps and torn earth where trees stood. Among the ruin, a
**mole family** huddles, their burrow crushed, a tiny mole pup trembling.
- Wisp: *(soft, stricken gasp.)* Bramble's ears fall.
- **Worry loop posed:** *why did the forest go quiet — and can it wake?*

**REACH (2:15–3:45)** — *a tentative, hopeful motif; lantern flickers.*
Bramble kneels, opens his **repair satchel**, gently lifts the mole pup into his
cloak. Wisp lifts the **lantern** high to light the grey — and in its glow they spot
one thing alive: a **single green sprout** in the ash. *(the hope metaphor planted.)*
Bramble cups it. First obstacle: night is coming, the ground is bare and cold, and
there are too many displaced creatures for two small friends to shelter.

**EFFORT & SETBACK (3:45–5:30)** — *low, uncertain strings; light fades; lantern near-dark.*
They try: Bramble hauls bark to build a windbreak; it **collapses**. Wisp tries to
warm the shivering moles with the lantern, but its light is **guttering low**. Rain
starts. The sprout bends under a cold drop. The duo, soaked and small against the
ruined grey, look lost. Bramble pulls Wisp close. *(the low point — almost silent
but for rain.)*

**TENDERNESS (5:30–6:45)** — *a single held note → a slow, rising swell; warmth returns.*
Wisp, shivering, still tips the **last of the lantern's glow** toward the mole pup
to keep it warm — a tiny, selfless act. The pup nuzzles in. Then: a soft light at
the treeline. **Bo the beetle** — and behind him, **every creature the duo helped
all season** (the sparrow, the freed beaver, the fox kit's family, the **firefly
swarm**) — arrive. *(KINDNESS-ECHO DETONATES.)* The fireflies pour into the lantern;
it **re-kindles, bright and warm.**

**RESTORATION (6:45–8:00)** — *the full settled theme; rich gold.*
Together they build a snug new burrow, lay bark shelters, and **plant the sprout** —
then a whole row of saplings, each watered from the mended stream. The moles are
safe and warm; the grey edge now holds tiny green promises glowing in lantern-light.
Bramble and Wisp **touch foreheads / double head-pat.** The lantern glows its
brightest as the Glen settles into a golden dusk.
- **END-CARD (optional, localizable/icon-first):** "It can take 100 years for a forest to grow back — but it starts with one seed." 🌱

**Shorts pulls:** (a) 2:00 the reveal of the broken forest + Wisp's gasp; (b) 5:45 Wisp giving the last glow to the mole pup; (c) 6:20 the kindness-echo arrival + lantern re-kindling.

### EP 1 — THE LIGHT IN THE HOLLOW  (~7 min, origin) — beat summary
- **CALM:** Bramble lives alone in Hollow Glen, mends broken things for the Chorus; shares his last honey-drop with **Bo the beetle**. *(kindness-echo planted, series-wide.)*
- **RIPPLE:** a storm; under a toadstool he finds a tiny, half-frozen **snowy owlet** (Wisp) beside a cracked, dark glass **lantern**. The lantern won't light.
- **REACH:** Bramble warms Wisp in his cloak; sets out to find a spark for the lantern; wonder beat — the night forest aglow.
- **EFFORT & SETBACK:** every spark fails; Wisp weakens; the lantern stays dark; lowest point in the cold.
- **TENDERNESS:** Bramble curls around Wisp, sharing warmth and the last of his honey; **Bo returns with the firefly swarm** — one firefly settles into the lantern. It glows. Wisp's eyes open.
- **RESTORATION:** dawn; Wisp, well, spirals around Bramble; first **forehead-touch / double head-pat**; the lantern glows warm. A duo, and a Glen, are born. 🌅

## How this was generated (so you can repeat it)
1. Picked a **calm Protector** (bear cub) + a **curious Wonder** (snowy owlet) from the cozy casting tables (`02`).
2. Invented a **signature prop that's also merch** + an emotional barometer (the firefly lantern).
3. Set a **silent ritual** as the wordless catchphrase (forehead-touch / double pat).
4. Planted a **series-wide kindness-echo** (honey for Bo → the swarm repays them) — the cozy "poetic justice."
5. Named a **cozy world** (Hollow Glen) and a **Gentle Foil** (Mr. Pemberly) for lived-in texture.
6. Led the season with the **nature pillar** and put the biggest swing on **Ep 6 "The Broken Forest"** (the competitor's proven 24M theme), engineered around **effort, not magic**.
7. Mapped every episode onto the **6-beat Comfort Arc** and wrote each with the per-episode template (`03`), wordless (`05`), graded warm-dominant (`04`), produced + localized via (`06`).
