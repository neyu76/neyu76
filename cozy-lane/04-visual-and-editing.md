# 04 — Visual Style Guide & Editing (Cozy Lane)

Lock these so every cozy series looks like one studio, AI generations stay
consistent, and the look itself becomes part of the **moat** (a recognizable
"studio fingerprint" copycats can't casually reproduce).

This is deliberately **different from `/studio-bible/04`**: where the revenge lane
is fast, hard-cut, and grade-flips cold↔gold for catharsis, the cozy lane is
**slow, soft, and warm-dominant** for reassurance.

---

## Visual identity

- **Render style:** 3D animated, **soft and plush** — rounded proportions, thick
  soft fur/felt texture, gentle subsurface scattering, **shallow depth of field**,
  volumetric golden light, dust-motes and floating spores for atmosphere. Think
  *Pixar-cozy but softer* — a world you want to nap in.
- **Aspect ratio:** **16:9 (primary)** for long-form + bedtime; **9:16** pulls for Shorts.
- **Framing:** characters small-to-medium in lush, layered environments (nature is a
  character). Leave headroom and negative space — it reads as calm.
- **Character consistency:** lock each character's design token + signature accent +
  the **signature prop** and reuse the exact string in every prompt (see `06`).

### Costume/accent = identity (cozy version)
- **Protector:** earthy, sturdy accent (woven grass-cloak, a satchel, a scarf) — never sharp.
- **Wonder:** one bright soft accent + the **signature prop** in-hand.
- **Guest-in-need:** designed for sympathy — droop, wide eyes, a small bandage once helped.
- Keep all silhouettes **round and plush-readable** (see `02`).

---

## Color & light = emotion (warm-dominant)

Half the storytelling. Unlike the revenge lane, the cozy lane **lives in warmth**
and only *dips* cool briefly, then returns to gold.

| Beat | Palette | Light | Notes |
|------|---------|-------|-------|
| **CALM** | warm amber, soft green, honey | golden hour, soft backlight, god-rays | the "home" baseline |
| **RIPPLE** | gently desaturated, cool-blue creeping in | overcast, softer light | sadness arriving — *dip, don't slam* |
| **REACH / WONDER** | luminous greens, teal-and-gold, bioluminescence | magic-hour glow, sparkle | the awe beat |
| **EFFORT & SETBACK** | muted, dusky, cool | low light, long shadows (still soft) | the low point — coolest the episode gets |
| **TENDERNESS** | warmth returning, amber bloom | a single warm source (the prop) leading | the turn — let warmth *grow* |
| **RESTORATION** | rich gold, saturated green, glow | sunset, lens warmth, prop glowing brightest | the payoff |

Rule of thumb: **warm = safe, cool = worry, glow = healed.** The arc is a slow
*cool-dip-and-return-to-gold*, not the revenge lane's hard cold↔gold flip. Let the
**signature prop's light** lead the eye through the emotional curve.

---

## Camera language (slow & gentle)

- **Slow moves only:** gentle push-ins, slow pans/tilts, soft parallax. **No
  whip-pans, no shake, no snap-zooms.**
- **Let shots settle:** hold 4–8s; allow a beautiful frame to simply *be*.
- **Close-ups for tenderness:** the Wonder's awe, the guest's relief, paws on fur.
  At least one close-up of the guest's face per episode.
- **Wides for safety & wonder:** lush establishing wides sell the cozy world and
  the scale of nature (hold up to ~3s — longer than the revenge lane allows).
- **Eye-level or gentle low angle** on the duo (never loom over them).
- **Insert shots** of the **signature prop** and the **Chorus critter** (sets up the kindness-echo).

---

## Editing rules (the comfort tempo)

- **Mostly hard cuts**, but **soft dissolves are allowed** for time-passing /
  dream / day→night (the revenge lane forbids these; the cozy lane needs them).
- **Cut on calm, not on impact:** cut on a settled breath or a musical phrase, not
  on a jolt. The edit should feel like *exhaling*.
- **Pacing:** shots ~4–8s; slow the cutting further toward RESTORATION and in
  bedtime compilations.
- **Soft cross-cutting:** you may intercut the duo's effort with the guest's
  waiting — gently, to build the *worry* loop, never dread.
- **Hold the final frame** 2–4s on the golden, peaceful close — the rewatch/loop hook.
- **No flash, no fast strobing** (sleep/kid-safe).

---

## Sound design (carries the story — see `05` for the full method)

With no dialogue, **sound is half the film.**

- **Music-led:** warm strings, soft piano, gentle harp/lullaby; an orchestral swell
  at TENDERNESS, a settling theme at RESTORATION.
- **Leitmotif per lead** + a **signature chime for the prop** (e.g., the lantern's
  soft *ting*) that cues callbacks before the image does.
- **Rich nature ambience & foley:** wind in leaves, distant water, soft footsteps,
  rustles, a heartbeat in the tender beat.
- **Non-verbal vocalizations** (chirps, gasps, hums) instead of words.
- **Mix for sound-on reward *and* low-volume bedtime** (nothing harsh or spiky).

---

## Thumbnail system (adopt the competitor's best move, brand it as ours)

- **No text overlay** — pure image; clean, premium, cinematic.
- **Legible emotion on the Wonder/guest** — readable feeling without the title.
- **Always a "guest" subject** (the new animal) → curiosity + novelty each episode.
- **Warm palette + golden light** → signals "safe & cozy" at a glance.
- **The signature prop visible** where natural → builds brand recognition across thumbnails.
- **High render detail** (soft fur, wet eyes, bounce light) → high perceived value.
- Keep a **consistent composition recipe** (duo + guest, warm light, shallow DOF) so
  the channel grid looks unmistakably ours.

---

## Consistency checklist across episodes & series

- [ ] Same soft render style + warm-dominant grade every episode.
- [ ] Each character's design + accent + prop identical to the bible (token reused verbatim).
- [ ] Signature prop tracks the emotion (rest → dim → glow) and looks identical each appearance.
- [ ] Grade follows the cool-dip-return-to-gold curve, not a hard flip.
- [ ] Pacing breathes (4–8s shots); soft dissolves only for time-passing.
- [ ] Thumbnail recipe consistent: no text, emotive face, guest subject, warm light, prop visible.
- [ ] Leitmotifs + prop chime reused across the series.
- [ ] 16:9 master + 9:16 Shorts pulls exported; same master serves all languages.
