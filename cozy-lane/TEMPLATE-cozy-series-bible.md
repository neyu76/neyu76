## 4. The through-line (the glue — see `cozy-lane/01`)
- **Signature prop (merch keystone + emotional barometer):** [e.g., firefly lantern] — states: rest/glow → dim → near-dark → re-kindle → brightest.
- **Silent ritual (catchphrase replacement):** [e.g., touch foreheads / double head-pat] — appears in CALM and again in RESTORATION.
- **Kindness-echo (the cozy "poetic justice"):** plant in CALM = [tiny kindness to a Chorus critter] → detonate in TENDERNESS = [how it returns to help them].
- **The episode promise:** every episode ends **safe & warm** (the audience contract).

## 5. The 6-beat Comfort Arc — per episode (see `cozy-lane/01`)

| Beat | Function | Grade/light (see `04`) | Prop state |

## 7. Visual & sound notes (see `cozy-lane/04` + `05`)
- Palette curve: warm → brief cool-dip → return to gold (never a hard flip).
- Pacing: slow, 4–8s shots; soft dissolves only on time-passing.
- Leitmotifs: Protector = [instrument/phrase] · Wonder = [instrument/phrase] · Prop = [signature chime].
- Signature inserts: the prop; the Chorus critter; one visual metaphor for hope.
- Thumbnail recipe: no text, emotive guest face, warm light, prop visible.
