In Wyoming, if you hear something small moving between the rocks at night... don't follow it into the dark.

Because in the old stories of the Shoshone, those mountains were never empty.

They call them the Nimerigar. The little people.

In the 1930s, two gold prospectors blasted open a cave in the Pedro Mountains. Inside, sitting cross-legged on a ledge, was a mummified figure barely fourteen inches tall. Years later, it disappeared.

According to the legend, the Nimerigar were fierce little hunters of the Wind River Mountains. They fired tiny poisoned arrows, and their name is often translated as "people eaters."

But here's the terrifying part. You'd never see them coming. You'd only hear the arrows.

They say they showed no mercy, not even to their own. When one of them grew too old or too sick, the others ended it with a single blow.

So if you're ever camping alone in those mountains tonight, and you hear tiny footsteps circling your tent, don't open it.

Because the one they found in that cave... was never the only one.
