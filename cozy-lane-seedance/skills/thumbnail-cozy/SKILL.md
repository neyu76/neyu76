## 📐 THE 6 COZY PATTERNS

🅰️ **EMOTIVE GUEST FACE** (default / highest CTR) — extreme close-up of the guest's worried/hopeful face, big glistening eyes, in its trouble (shrinking pool, torn wing). Lantern soft-glow to one side. *Use when the guest is irresistibly cute/vulnerable.*

🅱️ **DUO RESCUE MOMENT** — Bramble + Wisp reaching toward/cradling the guest mid-rescue; tender contact; warm light. *Use to show the heart of the episode (the help).*

🅲️ **WORRY-LOOP SPLIT** — soft diagonal split: warm safe side (duo + lantern) vs cool trouble side (world-force: dry dam, storm, ice). Guest in the middle. *Use when the stakes are visual.*

🅳️ **PROP-GLOW HERO** — the firefly lantern as hero, glowing bright, the duo small and cozy around it; guest peeking. *Use for wonder/atmosphere episodes (fog, night).*

🅴️ **WONDER BEAT** — Wisp's huge amber eyes reflecting something magical (crystals, fireflies, blooming color). *Use for the wonder pillar / strong Shorts crossover.*

🅵️ **BEFORE-AFTER RESTORATION** — split or implied contrast: grey/wilting vs blooming/golden (meadow grey->color, dry->flowing). *Use for the big Nature swings.*

> Always pick **3 DIFFERENT patterns** so the user can A/B test.

## 🚨 FAILURE MODES
1. Watercolor / cinematic-dark / flat-2D / photoreal style = FAILURE (must be soft 3D plush cozy).
2. Scary or distressing guest (not gentle vulnerable) = FAILURE (wrong lane).
3. Missing the firefly lantern (brand anchor) = FAILURE.
4. Text covering the emotive eyes/face = FAILURE.
5. Only 1 version per variation (must have V1 with-text + V2 no-text) = FAILURE.
6. No Canva specs for the no-text version = FAILURE.
7. Missing the dedicated @rorododotales-lookalike prompt = FAILURE.
8. Not 3 DISTINCT patterns = FAILURE.

## 🎯 PRO TIPS
- The vulnerable cute GUEST face is the click; the duo + lantern = brand recognition. Put both in.
- Brand recipe = NO TEXT thumbnails (matches @rorododotales) — but keep a V1 text option to A/B test.
- Glossy wet-eye highlight + warm rim light = the "aww" trigger; emphasize it every time.
- Reuse the lantern + warm-gold grade across every thumb so the channel is recognizable in-feed.
- One emotive subject beats a busy scene — cozy CTR rewards a single clear feeling.
