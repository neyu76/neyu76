**CORE FEATURE 7 — 6-BEAT COMFORT ARC.** CALM → RIPPLE → REACH → EFFORT & SETBACK →
TENDERNESS → RESTORATION. Tender-pull hook (not cruelty). Ends safe & warm; may tease the
next guest — never end on dread.

**CORE FEATURE 8 — SIGNATURE-PROP BAROMETER + KINDNESS-ECHO + SILENT RITUAL.** Prop's light
tracks emotion (glow→dim→near-dark→re-kindle→brightest); a small kindness planted in CALM
pays off as the rescue in TENDERNESS; a wordless ritual recurs in CALM + RESTORATION.

**CORE FEATURE 9 — WARM-DOMINANT GRADE.** Default warm amber-green golden hour; brief cool

**CORE FEATURE 10 — LOCALIZE-FIRST PACKAGE.** Master render is wordless, so one clip set
