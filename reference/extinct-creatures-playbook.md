# Playbook: "Creatures That Actually Existed" — AI Extinct/Prehistoric Micro-Doc

> The strategy + format bible for the **extinct/prehistoric** content line.
> Same proven engine as `reference/living-earth-tv-breakdown.md` (paradox hook → reveal → fact drop),
> but aimed at a niche where **AI is the only camera that can exist**.
> Powers `production-prompts/MASTER-PROMPT-extinct.md` and the `topic-/script-extinct-creatures` skills.

---

## 0. Why this niche WINS (the moat)

AI wildlife channels (LivingEarthTV etc.) still get "is this AI / fake?" in the comments because a **real** version of the footage exists to compare against. Extinct creatures have **no real footage and never can** — so:

- **AI photoreal becomes a feature, not a liability.** There is no "real" clip to be accused of faking.
- **The subject is inherently awe-inducing** ("this actually existed") and **universal + language-light + safe**.
- **Massive dual audience:** kids love dinosaurs; adults love the "I didn't know that was real" fact.
- **Evergreen + near-infinite catalog:** thousands of extinct species, each a video.

**Risk to manage:** paleontology has more uncertainty than living wildlife. The whole brand promise is credibility, so we tag every claim and hedge the narration (see §5). Misinformation is the one thing that kills this niche.

**Brand/series label:** "Creatures That Actually Existed" / "Monsters of the Past" (playlist), recurring narrator, a fixed CTA.

## I. Content Framework — "Mystery → Reveal → Scale/Fact Drop"

```
[Impossible-sounding claim] → [Creature name + era] → [the ONE huge number] → [how/why] → [CTA/loop]
```

A curiosity-gap loop identical to the wildlife format, with one signature device: the **SCALE ANCHOR** (see §III).

### Hook archetypes (pick one per video)
| Type | Pattern | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Scale/superlative** | "[creature] was bigger/heavier than [familiar thing]" | "This dinosaur was longer than a Boeing 737." |
| **Weird-anatomy / tool** | "[creature] had [impossible body part]" | "This shark had a buzzsaw for a jaw." |
| **Dark / apex** | "[creature] hunted [other scary thing]" | "This bird hunted baby T. rex." |
| **It-was-real** | "This actually existed: [creature]" | "A dragonfly the size of a hawk was real." |

## II. Video Structure (30–60s, 9:16 vertical)

| Time | Beat | What happens |
|------|------|--------------|
| 0–3s | **HOOK** | The impossible claim (ALL-CAPS overlay) over a photoreal reconstruction; no narrator name-drop yet. |
| 3–6s | **REVEAL** | Hard cut to a face/full-body shot: "THIS IS THE [NAME]" + era ("75 million years ago"). |
| 6–30s+ | **FACT + THE NUMBER** | The single jaw-dropping stat (size / weight / bite force / age) anchored to a familiar object. |
| mid | **MECHANISM** | How/why it worked (the hunt, the anatomy) + the slow-motion action burst. |
| end | **CTA / LOOP** | "...and it ruled the Earth for [X] million years. Follow for the next one." or clean loop. |

Caption format: line 1 = hook; lines 2–3 = the fact + the number ("scientists estimate..."); hashtags broad (`#dinosaurs #prehistoric #paleontology`) + species-specific.

## III. The SCALE ANCHOR (signature device — use in every video)

Numbers alone don't land; **comparison** does. Every video anchors the creature's size to something the viewer knows:
- human silhouette · school bus · giraffe · Boeing 737 · basketball hoop · car · a person's hand.
- Show it **visually** (a to-scale modern object in frame or a cut-away) AND **say it** ("longer than two school buses").
- This is the share-trigger: "wait, it was THAT big?"

---

## IV. Visual & Motion (photoreal paleo-reconstruction — NOT cartoon)

- **Style:** "Prehistoric Planet" / "Walking with Dinosaurs" realism — looks like a real wildlife camera somehow filmed the Mesozoic.
- **Accuracy in the render (see §5):** feathered raptors/tyrannosauroids where consensus supports it, correct upright posture, no dragging tails, no pronated "bunny hands," plausible (flagged-speculative) colors.
- **Composition:** extreme close-ups of the eye/teeth (stop-scroll) + one full-body shot WITH a scale anchor.
- **Camera:** static or slow push-in; the creature moves, the camera barely does. No shaky cam, no jump-cuts.
- **Depth:** shallow DOF on close-ups; medium-shallow on full-body (habitat reads but stays soft).
- **Speed:** smooth 60fps; slow-motion on the action burst (the bite, the wing-spread, the charge).
- **Grade:** earthy warm haze for land (Cretaceous dawn dust), cold blue-green for ancient oceans, white-cold for Ice Age.
- **Transitions:** hard cut on the narration rhythm · slow zoom-in · still-frame → sudden movement · text fade-in.
- **Audio:** ambient prehistoric soundscape (insects, wind, distant calls) + cinematic score; creatures make natural roars/calls ONLY — they never "talk" or lip-sync to narration.

## V. Scientific-Accuracy Framework (the credibility moat)

Tag EVERY claim, and frame the narration to match the tag:

| Tag | Meaning | Narration framing |
|-----|---------|-------------------|
| `verified` | well-established fossil fact | state plainly ("it had 60 teeth") |
| `estimate` | size/weight/force inferred | "scientists estimate..." / "around..." |
| `reconstruction` | appearance/behavior inferred | "fossils suggest..." / "likely..." |
| `debated` | contested among experts | "some scientists think..." / avoid if shaky |

**Hard rules:**
- Never present an `estimate`/`debated` claim as certain fact.
- Avoid debunked tropes (scaly Velociraptor with no feathers; megalodon = just a giant great white; Spinosaurus as a simple T. rex clone; dinosaurs as sluggish tail-draggers).
- Prefer the creature whose ESTABLISHED fact is already the hook — real prehistory is unbelievable enough.
- One memorable number per video; if it's an estimate, say so (it actually *increases* trust).

---

## VI. Pacing — WPM (inherited from the wildlife line)

- **Band 120–165 WPM, default ~135.** Slower (~125) for suspenseful apex-predator builds; faster (~160) for stacking shock facts on a low-movement creature.
- `N_SCENES = round(LENGTH / 10)` · `NARRATION_BUDGET = (LENGTH/60) × 135` words.
- Lines 5–12 words; hook in scene 1; hold the final frame ~1.5s (loop/CTA).

## VII. Sample hooks (real, taggable facts — never reuse a competitor's exact creature back-to-back)

- "This shark had a spiral saw for a jaw." — Helicoprion (`reconstruction`: jaw position debated → frame carefully)
- "This dinosaur was longer than a blue whale." — Argentinosaurus (`estimate`: ~30–35 m)
- "A flying reptile stood as tall as a giraffe." — Quetzalcoatlus (`estimate`: ~10–11 m wingspan, ~5 m standing)
- "This 'bird' was taller than a man and couldn't fly." — terror bird / Phorusrhacos (`reconstruction`)
- "A dragonfly the size of a hawk ruled the skies." — Meganeura (`verified`: ~65–70 cm wingspan)
- "This sea monster had teeth like bananas." — Megalodon (`estimate`: tooth ~18 cm; bite force estimated, `debated`)
- "This crocodile ate dinosaurs." — Deinosuchus / Sarcosuchus (`estimate`: ~10–12 m)
- "This 'sloth' was the size of an elephant." — Megatherium (`verified`/`estimate`)
- "This snake could swallow a crocodile whole." — Titanoboa (`estimate`: ~12–13 m, ~1 tonne)
- "This shark-lizard hunted in the open ocean." — Mosasaurus (`reconstruction`)

## VIII. Clone/launch blueprint

```
Niche:        one extinct creature per video (standalone), playlist "Creatures That Actually Existed"
Hook:         "[creature] was bigger than [X]" / "[creature] had [impossible part]" / "this actually existed"
Scale anchor: always compare size to a familiar modern object (visual + spoken)
AI tools:     Kling / Veo / Runway / Sora (motion) · Midjourney/Flux/Nano-Banana (bait image) · ChatGPT (paleo research)
Edit:         CapCut — ALL CAPS bold white text, hard cuts, slow zoom-ins, the number as an overlay
Length:       30–45s sweet spot · narrator 120–165 WPM, single locked voice
Accuracy:     tag every claim (verified/estimate/reconstruction/debated); hedge narration; no debunked tropes
Label:        mark upload AI-generated; optional "paleo-reconstruction, scientifically informed" note
Post rate:    1–2/day; double down on the creature types that pop
```

**One-line summary:** A photoreal AI paleo-documentary micro-format that wins because the subject can ONLY be made with AI, pairing an unbelievable-but-real prehistoric fact with a scale anchor and a calm narrator — awe + credibility + infinite catalog.
