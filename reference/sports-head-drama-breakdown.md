## 0. The niche in one line

**`Sports fandom conflict + family/orphan melodrama + cliffhanger`**, rendered as
Pixar-tier 3D where every character has a **sports ball for a head** (basketball,
football) on a realistic human body wearing official team colors. The ball-head
is the unmistakable, ownable thumbnail signature.

### Content & genre
- **Genre:** AI-generated animated drama short film, **serialized** (Part 1, Part 2…).
- **Core themes:** family conflict around sports allegiance (Eagles dad vs Cowboys-fan son; Lakers family vs Celtics-loving kid), betrayal, internal shame.
- **Format:** ~1:30 to ~2:39 per video (longer than the TikTok average), split into Parts to pull viewers back.
- **Captions:** short, drama-forward — e.g. *"Betrayed Lakers Family | Part1"*, *"Eagles Family War vs Cowboys Boyfriend | Part 1"*.
- **Hashtags:** `#aistory #sadstory #basketball #nfl #fyp #brainrot` — targets sports fandoms + AI-content audiences.

### Storytelling formula
`Fanship conflict + family drama + cliffhanger`
1. **Hook (0–10s):** peak tension immediately — discovery of betrayal, confrontation, or a shock reveal.
2. **Rising action:** the argument, layered emotions.
3. **Emotional peak:** a character cries, rages, or goes silent in pain.
4. **Cliffhanger / "to be continued."**

**Dialogue:** colloquial English (AAVE-flavored), short, raw, emotional — e.g. *"VANESSA WHOSE MONEY"*, *"I'VE BEEN BUSTING"*, *"EXPLAIN YOU BROUGHT [a Cowboys jersey]"*, *"A CELTICS JERSEY"*.

### Visual art & style
- **Style:** 3D AI cinematic animation, high-quality render, near Pixar/DreamWorks but fully AI-made.
- **Signature character design:** **the head is a sports ball** (basketball, football) instead of a human head — the channel's unique brand identifier. Body is a realistic human (brown skin, realistic build) in official NBA/NFL kits. Expressions are highly readable (anger, shock, sadness, fear) despite the ball head. Kids are smaller/chibi/cuter; women have hair, jewelry, body curves — still ball-headed.
- **Lighting & color:** dramatic cinematic lighting, heavy chiaroscuro; team colors dominate the set (all-blue Cowboys room, Lakers purple, Eagles green). "Movie quality," not typical TikTok.
- **Settings:** family living room (TV, sofa), kid's bedroom (decorated for favorite team), run-down house (betrayal drama), stadium, hospital, kitchen — story-dependent.

## 3. aistory.us 10-second hook blueprint

```
[0s]    -> IN MEDIAS RES: no intro, no context — drop straight into emotion
[0-3s]  -> VISUAL SHOCK: max contrast (big vs small / light vs dark / warm vs cold)
[3-7s]  -> CHARACTER SYMPATHY: close-up on the weak character's face -> protective reflex
[7-10s] -> TENSION SEED: 1-2 subtitle words hint at conflict but DON'T explain
          -> viewer's brain asks the question -> must keep watching
```

**Conclusion:** aistory.us runs a classic soap-opera / telenovela structure with an
AI "brainrot" aesthetic — extremely effective for serialized TikTok, evidenced by
an unusually high save/view rate (P1 ≈ 0.39% save rate, ~2× the industry average).

## 4. Combined takeaways — how to produce similar videos

These map 1:1 onto our existing engine (`studio-bible/01–06`). Only the **skin** changes.

1. **Open at peak conflict or peak loneliness.** Two reliable cold-opens:
   (a) a confrontation already exploding (FilmVibe), or (b) a lone abandoned child in the dark (aistory.us). Establish the situation in ≤3s.
2. **Keep the hook to 1–3 characters** so emotion reads. Save the 4–5 character crowd for the climax.
3. **Ball-head = the moat.** Lock a design token: sports ball head (with/without team logo) + realistic human body + official team colors. Kids = smaller/chibi.
4. **Color-code the sides** so viewers instantly know who's who (team A color vs team B color dominates each set).
5. **Use a curiosity-gap subtitle.** ALL CAPS, white + black stroke (or yellow highlight), word-by-word to the voice beat; cut the final line off (*"ANYMORE", "BUSTING", "YOU BROUGHT"*).
6. **Lantern/chiaroscuro lighting** for the sad beats; warm amber for hope; cold blue for cruelty.
7. **Serialize with cliffhangers** every part; end Part N at max tension to farm "Part N+1" comments.
8. **Pick a real fandom rivalry** as the engine (Lakers/Celtics, Eagles/Cowboys, Thunder/Spurs). The allegiance *is* the conflict — built-in stakes + a passionate audience. (Keep teams fictionalized/disclosed for monetized work.)
9. **A protected child anchor** (orphan / bullied kid) makes the karma righteous — same lever as the Pup in "Dog Swings Alone".
10. **130–145 WPM, hard cuts, 9:16, ~10s clips, burned-in captions** — same production spec as the rest of the bible.

### Strengths to emulate
- Genuinely **unique visual** (nobody else does sports-head drama at quality).
- **Razor-sharp niche:** US sports fans (NBA + NFL) ∩ AI-content audience.
- Series format → retention + a loyal fanbase.
- High render quality → premium feel.
- Rich, readable expressions despite the ball head.

### Gaps = our opportunity
- Both sit in the 32K–64K range — **consistency/cadence** is the lever to grow.
- View counts are uneven (some 13K–49K) — tighter hooks + better topic selection.
- **No localization** (English only) → an opening to localize (see `markets/latam/`).
