## 3. AVATAR / LOGO CONCEPT
The face should signal "emotional human stories" and read at tiny size without
text. A child's silhouette against a warm window is the genre in one image.

**Avatar image prompt (1:1, generate then crop to circle):**
```
A cinematic photoreal silhouette of a small child holding a worn teddy bear,
seen from behind, looking up at a single warm-lit window on a cold blue snowy
street. Soft amber rim light on the child against a deep blue-grey background.
Emotional, film-grain, shallow depth of field. Bold readable silhouette,
centered, no text. --ar 1:1
```
- **Variants:** a faint warm glow / a single falling snowflake / a small heart
  of light in the window to hint the hopeful turn.
- **Color system:** cold blue-grey (the drama) + one warm amber point (the hope).
  Reuse on title cards + captions for a recognizable look (anti-copycat moat).
- **Profile-pic rule:** the silhouette fills ~80% of the circle; high contrast;
  no words (words vanish at thumbnail size).

**Banner/header (16:9) prompt (YouTube):**
```
Cinematic photoreal banner: on the left, a small child in an oversized coat
stands alone in falling snow on a grey street; on the right, a warm-lit diner
window glows amber. Dramatic cold-vs-warm lighting, 35mm film look, shallow
depth of field, space in the center for the channel name. NOT animated,
no text baked in. --ar 16:9
```

## 5. LAUNCH CHECKLIST & CADENCE
- [ ] Reserve the SAME handle on TikTok / YouTube / Instagram.
- [ ] Avatar + banner generated; title-card template + caption font locked (the look = your moat).
- [ ] **Turn ON the platform AI-generated label** on every upload (US requirement).
- [ ] Batch a FULL series (all parts) before posting Part 1 — never break a cliffhanger streak.
- [ ] Post daily or every other day at a fixed US time (7-9pm ET); pin Part 1; one playlist per series.
- [ ] First comment teases the next part; reply in-English to early comments to boost reach.
- [ ] Calendar tie-ins: Mother's Day, Father's Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas, first snow.
- [ ] Keep characters fictional; no real people/cases; hopeful tone; never graphic.
