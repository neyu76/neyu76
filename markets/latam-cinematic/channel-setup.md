# Channel Setup — South America · Cinematic Drama (es-419 / pt-BR)

Identity kit for the South American **cinematic-drama** channels (pure drama +
visual art, NOT the viral brand). Recommendation: **two channels** — one Spanish
(`es-419`), one Brazilian Portuguese (`pt-BR`) — same content, localized separately.

> Different brand from `markets/latam/channel-setup.md`. This one leans
> "emotional cinema": tasteful, tear-jerker, beautiful — not loud/viral.

## 3. AVATAR / LOGO (the capybara mascot — humble hero)
**Avatar prompt (1:1, crop to circle):**
```
A gentle 3D animated capybara mascot, head-and-shoulders, Illumination/Pixar style,
warm kind eyes glistening with quiet emotion, a tiny worn straw hat or a small marigold
behind the ear, soft golden-hour rim light, simple deep-teal-to-gold gradient background,
bold clean silhouette readable at tiny size, centered, no text. --ar 1:1
```
- Color system: warm amber + earthy brown (heart) with a cold teal accent (the drama) and a golden-hour glow (restoration). Reuse on title cards + captions = your moat.
- Profile-pic rule: face fills ~80% of the circle; high contrast; no words.

**Banner (16:9):**
```
Cinematic banner: a humble capybara in worker overalls stands small in golden-hour light
on the left; a looming jaguar tycoon silhouette in cold shadow on the right; a tiny wooden
swing motif in the foreground; warm-vs-cold lighting; center space for the channel name.
3D Illumination style, no text baked in. --ar 16:9
```
