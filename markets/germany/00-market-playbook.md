# Germany Market Playbook — CINEMATIC DRAMA (pure drama + visual art)

Localization layer that pivots the **Cinematic Drama** pipeline (the pure-drama /
visual-art / 4-Act branch) to the **German market (`de-DE`)**. The story/visual
engine in `production-prompts/PIPELINE-drama-cinematic.md` is UNCHANGED — this
playbook only swaps **language, cast fauna, names, sincere insults, settings,
music, captions, and voice locale**. The `*-drama-cinematic-de` skills + the German
cinematic master prompt reference this file.

## 0. WHY CINEMATIC DRAMA WINS IN GERMANY (the thesis)
Germany **invented** the moral talking-animal tale: the Brothers Grimm, *Reineke
Fuchs* (the cunning fox), *Die Bremer Stadtmusikanten*. Audiences grew up on
**Märchen** — clear good vs. evil, a wronged innocent, and the corrupt getting
their just deserts. Our cinematic drama is the Grimm fable rebuilt as a beautiful,
muted-friendly animated short. We are serving a deeply native taste.

Cultural pillars to lean on:
- **Gerechtigkeit (justice)** — Germans deeply value fairness; the corrupt rich
  being held to account ("die gerechte Strafe") is profoundly satisfying.
- **Handwerk (honest craft/work)** — a humble, skilled worker is a respected hero;
  show the craft (building the house, carving the swing) lovingly.
- **Familie & Heimat** — family and a sense of home/belonging carry the heart.
- **Quality over slop** — German viewers are quick to reject cheap "AI slop." This
  branch's restraint, real emotion, and careful imagery are the right answer.

**No meme slang.** Forbidden: mogged/glow-up/"caught in 4K" and English meme talk.
Insults are sincere, plain German wounds (see §4). German is a strong **dubbing
culture** — native German voice (`de-DE`) is mandatory; never ship English audio.

## 1. LANGUAGE STRATEGY
- **Primary: `de-DE`** — Standard German (Hochdeutsch). Broadest reach (Germany, Austria, German-speaking Switzerland with neutral wording).
- Spoken dialogue = German. Vietnamese/English appear ONLY in the review phase as a gloss for the editor.
- **Pace note:** German compounds are long, so a line can *look* long; this branch already runs the slower DRAMA band (**100-130 WPM**) with silent Peaks. Keep lines to one breath (~4-8 words), prefer plain words over compounds in dialogue, and let the PEAK go nearly silent.
- Avoid heavy regional dialect in dialogue (keep it Hochdeutsch so all German speakers follow); regional flavor lives in the SETTINGS and MUSIC, not the words.

## 2. GERMAN / CENTRAL-EUROPEAN FAUNA CASTING TABLE (the visual differentiator)
Cast fauna from German forests and fairy tales so the world feels unmistakably German.

| Role | Best animals | Reads as |
|------|--------------|----------|
| **HERO** (loving worker/parent) | **Dachs/Badger** (hardworking digger-builder, beloved) · **Biber/Beaver** (builder) · Igel/Hedgehog (humble) · Esel/Donkey (Bremen tradition) | honest craftsman, underestimated |
| **TYRANT** (moneyed criminal) | **Fuchs/Fox** (Reineke — the classic German schemer) · **Wolf** (predator) · Luchs/Lynx · Wildschwein/Boar (brutish bully) | cunning, smooth, cruel |
| **BETRAYER** (materialistic insider) | **Elster/Magpie** ("diebische Elster" — loves shiny things/money) · Pfau/Peacock (vain) · Marder/Marten | glamorous, greedy, self-serving |
| **INNOCENT** (the child) | Rehkitz/Fawn · baby Dachs/Igel · Entlein/Duckling · Hasenkind/Bunny | pure, vulnerable anchor |
| **JUSTICE** (calm authority) | **Uhu/Eule (Owl)** — the wise judge · **Bär/Bear** — the steady detective (Kommissar) · Adler/Eagle (authority) | incorruptible authority |
| **ACCOMPLICE** (enables the crime) | Ratte/Rat · Krähe/Rabe (Crow/Raven) · Wiesel/Weasel · slick Marder (lawyer) | shifty, expendable / slippery |

Rules: one hero / one villain, never reuse an animal across two roles, match job to nature (Dachs builds, Fuchs schemes, Uhu judges, Elster hoards shiny money), distinct silhouettes, **≤15 assets/project**, assign a `@Handle` + a frozen voice profile to each.

> Signature mascot: the **Dachs (badger)** — the humble German craftsman-hero; the **Igel (hedgehog)** is a beloved gentle alternative for the channel face.

## 4. SINCERE INSULT KIT (NO meme slang — plain German wounds)
The insult to reverse is delivered straight, cold, and human — never a meme.
- "Du bist ein Niemand, [Name]. Ein Niemand." (You're a nobody.)
- "Du warst schon immer ein Versager." (You were always a failure/loser.)
- "Das geht dich nichts an." (This doesn't concern you.)
- "Du armer Tropf." (You poor wretch.) · "Träum weiter." (Keep dreaming.)
- Karma / justice lines (the satisfying turn): "Am Ende siegt die Gerechtigkeit." (In the end, justice wins.) · "Jetzt kommt die Rechnung." (Now comes the reckoning.) · "Das Schicksal holt jeden ein." (Fate catches everyone.)
- The silent reversal at the end is mechanic-identical: the "Niemand" now keeps the workshop, the home, the child.

## 5. SETTINGS + VISUAL SIGNATURE (pick one per series; this branch is visual-led)
Each series locks a **recurring motif** + an emotion→grade map + 2-3 signature shots.
- **Schwarzwald village** (Black Forest, Fachwerk half-timbered houses, pines) — motif: a hand-carved wooden swing / a cuckoo-clock · grade: warm lamp-amber vs cold forest blue.
- **Alpine foothill farm** (Bavaria/Alps, meadows, snow peaks) — motif: a wooden fence the father builds · grade: golden meadow vs grey storm.
- **Baltic/North-Sea fishing town** (red-brick, harbour, gulls) — motif: a small painted boat · grade: teal sea / amber dusk.
- **Ruhr workshop / Schrebergarten allotment** (honest industry, garden plots) — motif: the father's workbench / a single apple tree · grade: warm workshop light vs cold steel grey.
- **Snowy Weihnachtsmarkt town** (Christmas market, lights) — motif: a wooden toy the father makes · grade: warm market gold vs cold blue night.

Emotion→grade (locked across the branch): family = warm amber · villain = cold blue-gray · loss/rock-bottom = desaturated grey · karma/police = blue-red flash · restoration = glowing gold (golden hour). Signature shots recur every part (low-angle on the fox/wolf, macro tear on the child's drawing, slow-mo embrace, pull-back to the golden home).

## 6. MUSIC FLAVOR (emotional, with regional color)
- Loss/grief: solo cello or piano (German Romantic restraint); a Spieluhr (music box) for the child.
- Tension/scheming: low strings + a ticking clock motif.
- Alpine warmth: gentle Zither / accordion (used sparingly).
- Restoration/golden hour: warm strings rising, a single horn.
- Emotion first; **keep the PEAK nearly silent** — let SFX (a creaking swing, snow, a hammer, footsteps) and one instrument carry it.

## 7. UPLOAD / HASHTAGS / POSTING
- Title & captions in German. Pose the injustice as a question; emotion over clickbait.
- **de hashtags:** #geschichten #animation #karma #gerechtigkeit #familie #emotional #shorts #fürdich #märchen #tiere #herzzerreißend
- Calendar hooks: **Muttertag**, **Vatertag**, **Weihnachten** (huge — the Christmas-market series), **Ostern**, the cosy autumn/winter "cocooning" season.
- Cadence: batch a whole series, post daily/every other day at a fixed local time, pin Part 1, one playlist per series, first comment teases the next part.
- Quality note: Germans reward craft — clean captions, consistent grade, no glitches. The look IS the moat.
