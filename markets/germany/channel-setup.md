# Channel Setup — Germany · Cinematic Drama (de-DE)

Identity kit for the German **cinematic-drama** channel (pure drama + visual art,
Grimm/Reineke-Fuchs tradition). One channel, `de-DE`, reaching Germany + Austria +
German-speaking Switzerland.

> German viewers reward craft and reject "AI slop" — lean into restraint, real
> emotion, clean captions, a consistent grade. The fairy-tale clarity (Gut/Böse)
> is an advantage here, not a cliché.

## 3. AVATAR / LOGO (the Dachs/badger mascot — humble craftsman hero)
Der **Dachs (Badger)** ist der bescheidene deutsche Handwerker-Held; der **Igel
(Hedgehog)** ist die sanfte, beliebte Alternative als Kanalgesicht.

**Avatar-Prompt (1:1, kreisförmig zuschneiden):**
```
A gentle 3D animated badger (Dachs) mascot, head-and-shoulders, Illumination/Pixar style,
warm kind eyes with quiet emotion, small worker's cap or a pencil behind the ear, soft
golden-hour rim light, simple forest-blue-to-amber gradient background, bold clean
silhouette readable at tiny size, centered, no text. --ar 1:1
```
- Farbsystem: warmes Bernstein + Waldbraun (Herz/Handwerk) mit kaltem Blaugrau-Akzent (das Drama) und einem goldenen Schimmer (Wiederherstellung). Auf Title-Cards + Untertiteln wiederverwenden = der Moat.
- Profilbild-Regel: Gesicht füllt ~80% des Kreises; hoher Kontrast; keine Wörter.

**Banner (16:9):**
```
Cinematic banner: a humble badger in worker overalls stands small in golden-hour light by a
Black-Forest half-timbered house on the left; a looming fox tycoon silhouette in cold shadow
on the right; a small wooden swing motif in the foreground; warm-vs-cold lighting; center
space for the channel name. 3D Illumination style, no text baked in. --ar 16:9
```
