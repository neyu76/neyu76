## 3. AVATAR / LOGO CONCEPT (the capybara mascot)
The **capybara** is a beloved internet icon across South America — calm, humble,
universally liked. Use it as the channel face (it also fits the "humble hero").

**Avatar image prompt (1:1, generate then crop to circle):**
```
A friendly 3D animated capybara mascot head-and-shoulders, Illumination/Pixar style,
warm kind eyes, gentle confident smile, a tiny worn straw hat or a small marigold
behind the ear, soft warm amber rim light, simple deep-teal-to-gold gradient background,
bold clean silhouette readable at tiny size, centered, no text. --ar 1:1
```
- **Variants:** put a faint golden "karma" glow / a small paw-print + spark motif behind the head to hint the genre.
- **Color system:** warm amber + earthy brown (hero/heart) with a cold teal accent (the drama). Reuse on title cards + captions for a recognizable look (anti-copycat moat).
- **Profile-pic rule:** face fills ~80% of the circle; high contrast; no words (words vanish at thumbnail size).

**Banner/header (16:9) prompt (YouTube):**
```
Cinematic vertical-friendly banner: a humble capybara in worker overalls stands small
against a golden pampas/Andes sunset on the left; a looming jaguar tycoon silhouette in
shadow on the right; dramatic warm-vs-cold lighting; space in the center for the channel
name. 3D Illumination style, no text baked in. --ar 16:9
```
