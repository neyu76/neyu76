# LatAm Market Playbook — Animal Drama Saga (South America edition)

Localization layer that pivots the original "Dog Swings Alone" engine to the
**South American market**. The story engine in `studio-bible/01,03,04,05` is
UNCHANGED — this playbook only swaps language, cast fauna, names, slang, settings,
voices, and channel setup. The LatAm skills + master prompt reference this file.

---

## 0. WHY THIS FORMAT WINS IN LATAM (the thesis)
South America is the global home of the **telenovela**. The audience is already
trained to crave exactly our 7-beat engine: betrayal, class conflict, secret
heirs, family honor, revenge, and redemption. We are not introducing a new taste —
we serve a beloved one in a fresh, bingeable, AI-animated, muted-friendly short
form. The payoff "**le llegó el karma**" (karma caught up with him) is deeply
satisfying in this culture. That is the unfair advantage.

Cultural pillars to lean on:
- **La familia** is sacred — father/mother sacrifice and protecting a child = max emotional pull.
- **Justice & faith** — the righteous poor beating the corrupt rich is a cultural fantasy.
- **Fútbol / futebol**, street food, markets, Carnaval — texture and optional skins.

---

## 1. LANGUAGE STRATEGY
- **Primary (default): `es-419`** — neutral Latin American Spanish. Broadest reach (Argentina, Colombia, Peru, Chile, Venezuela, Ecuador, Bolivia, Uruguay, Paraguay + spillover).
- **Brazil: `pt-BR`** — Brazilian Portuguese. Brazil is the largest TikTok market in the region; treat it as its own channel.
- **Recommendation:** run TWO channels — one `es-419`, one `pt-BR`. Same scripts, localized separately (never mix Spanish and Portuguese in one video).
- Spoken language for all dialogue = the target locale. Vietnamese/English appear ONLY in the review phase as a gloss for the editor.
- **WPM note:** Spanish & Portuguese pack more syllables per word than English. Keep the same emotional pacing band but expect lines to *sound* ~10-15% longer; trim to keep the 130-145 WPM feel and readable captions. Lines stay short (one breath, ~4-9 words).

## 2. SOUTH-AMERICAN FAUNA CASTING TABLE (the visual differentiator)
Cast native/iconic South American animals so the world feels unmistakably LatAm.

| Role | Best LatAm animals | Reads as |
|------|--------------------|----------|
| **HERO** (humble worker/builder) | **Capybara/Carpincho/Capivara** (calm, hardworking, beloved icon) · Ox/Buey · Donkey/Burro · Llama · Armadillo/Tatú · Sloth/Perezoso · Anteater · Tapir/Anta | honest, gentle, underestimated |
| **TYRANT** (rich predator) | **Jaguar/Onça** · Caiman/Yacaré/Jacaré (loan shark, land-grabber) · Harpy Eagle/Águila Harpía · Puma · Anaconda (finance) · Ocelot | powerful, arrogant, cruel |
| **BETRAYER** (vain insider) | **Macaw/Guacamayo/Arara** (showy, vain) · Toucan/Tucán · Coati/Coatí · Flamingo · Margay | glamorous, self-serving |
| **INNOCENT** (cub) | baby capybara · llama cria · fawn · baby sloth · duckling · baby armadillo | pure, vulnerable anchor |
| **JUSTICE** (calm authority) | **Andean Condor** (majestic, sees all → judge) · Owl/Búho/Coruja · Spectacled Bear/Oso de Anteojos (detective) · good-Jaguar (chief) | incorruptible authority |
| **HENCHMAN** (leaks the secret) | Vulture/Urubú/Zopilote · Opossum/Zarigüeya/Gambá · Coati · Rat · Piranha | shifty, expendable |

Rules unchanged: one hero / one villain, never reuse an animal across two roles, match job to nature (capybara builds/farms, caiman does loans, condor judges, macaw flaunts), distinct silhouettes, ≤15 assets/project.

> Signature mascot pick: the **capybara** is a beloved internet icon across South America — ideal recurring hero and channel avatar.

## 6. MUSIC FLAVOR (emotional, with regional color)
- Sad/loss: Andean charango/quena, or Brazilian viola caipira, or bolero strings.
- Tension/scheming: low pulse + cuíca/berimbau accents (BR).
- Reunion/warmth: nylon guitar + warm strings.
- Triumph/rebirth: rising brass / cumbia-triunfal lift.
- Emotion first; regional instruments are seasoning, not a gimmick.
