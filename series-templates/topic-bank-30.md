# 30 Viral-Ready Topics — feed straight into the `script-animal-drama` skill

Each topic is engineered on the Story Engine: **underdog hero (a builder/worker)**
loses everything to an **arrogant predator villain**, is betrayed by a **vain
insider**, anchored by an **innocent cub**, and wins through **karma + a
poetic-justice twist** (the villain loses the exact thing they stole, to the
exact "loser" they mocked).

**How to use:** copy any block's `TOPIC:` line into the skill input. Default
`LENGTH 90-120s`, `MODE standalone`. For a series, run the same TOPIC in
`MODE pilot` (Part 1) then `MODE series-part` for each beat.

> Variety is intentional: 30 different animal casts, crafts, settings, and twist
> payloads (buried gold, a discarded masterpiece going viral, the villain's own
> crime, a hidden will, the despised thing turning priceless).

---

### 1. The Baker's Last Loaf
**TOPIC:** An owl single-dad village baker loses his shop and his late wife's secret recipe to a fox franchise mogul; his owlet's testimony plus the gold coins his wife sealed inside the "worthless" old brick oven let him buy the whole franchise back.
**Cast:** Owl baker (hero) · Fox franchise mogul (villain) · Magpie partner (betrayer) · owlet (innocent) · Bear inspector (justice). **Promise:** a birthday cake. **Twist:** gold in the oven wall.

**Cast:** Ox farmer · Crocodile loan shark · Fox brother-in-law (betrayer) · calf · Bear sheriff. **Promise:** a vegetable patch. **Twist:** the spring under the field.
