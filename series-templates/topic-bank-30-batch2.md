# 30 Viral-Ready Topics — Batch 2 (No. 31-60)

Theme-diversified follow-up to `topic-bank-30.md`: mother-child, siblings,
friendship betrayal, school/bullying, elderly & legacy, love/marriage betrayal
& redemption, plus high-concept. Each entry now adds a **MODE** recommendation
and a **Viral Tier**.

**MODE legend**
- 🎬 **STANDALONE** — one self-contained 90-120s video (full arc + poetic-justice button).
- 📺 **SERIES (N parts)** — multi-part; each part ends on a cliffhanger. Part summary included.

**Viral Tier legend**
- 🟣 **S** — "guaranteed": maximum primal emotion (sacrifice / orphan / close betrayal) + strong poetic justice + universal. ~5M+ ceiling.
- 🔵 **A** — "high": strong premise, needs clean execution. ~1-5M.
- 🟢 **B+** — "safe bet": wholesome/lower-stakes, softer payoff. ~200K-1M.

**How to use:** paste the `TOPIC:` line into the `script-animal-drama` skill.
For 📺 series, run `MODE pilot` for Part 1, then `MODE series-part` per part.

## 👩‍👧 MOTHER-CHILD (tình mẫu tử)

### 31. The Hospital Window  · 🎬/📺 · 🟣 S
**TOPIC:** A sparrow seamstress mother sells her wedding ring and works night shifts to pay for her chick's eye surgery, but a peacock hospital director diverts the donated fund to a rich patient; once healed, the chick's first painting — of his mother's worn hands — wins a contest that exposes the theft and funds a whole children's ward.
**Cast:** Sparrow mother (hero) · Peacock director (villain) · Pigeon administrator (betrayer) · chick (innocent) · Owl board chair (justice).
**MODE:** Standalone 110s, or 📺 3 parts — *The Sacrifice / The Stolen Fund / The Painting*.

### 32. Two Bowls  · 🎬 · 🔵 A
**TOPIC:** A hen runs a roadside rice stall and always gives her chick the bigger bowl; a rooster diner-chain boss bulldozes the stall and mocks her, until a clip of her feeding stray chicks makes her beloved and the city hands her the prime corner he wanted.
**Cast:** Hen cook (hero) · Rooster chain boss (villain) · Duck supplier (betrayer) · chick (innocent) · Owl councilwoman (justice).
**MODE:** Standalone 90s.

### 33. The Borrowed Name  · 📺 · 🟣 S
**TOPIC:** A wolf pup, abandoned for being "weak," is raised lovingly by a poor goat mother; the pack's alpha returns to seize her land, not knowing the pup he cast out is the heir the pack needs — and the pup chooses his goat mother and strips the alpha of his title.
**Cast:** Goat mother + wolf pup (heroes) · Alpha wolf (villain) · Jackal advisor (betrayer) · the pup (innocent) · Bear elder (justice).
**MODE:** 📺 4 parts — *The Abandonment / The Goat Mother / The Return / The Choice*. (Predator-raised-by-prey + identity reveal = huge binge.)

### 34. Her Last Song  · 🎬 · 🔵 A
**TOPIC:** A nightingale mother going deaf teaches her chick to sing before she loses her hearing; a peacock talent-show mogul humiliates them and steals the song — so at the finale the chick signs the lullaby so she can "see" it, the crowd turns on the mogul, and the song becomes an anthem.
**Cast:** Nightingale mother (hero) · Peacock mogul (villain) · Parrot judge (betrayer) · chick (innocent) · Owl host (justice).
**MODE:** Standalone 120s.

### 35. The Umbrella  · 🎬 · 🔵 A
**TOPIC:** A frog mother walks her tadpole to school under one broken umbrella, soaked herself so the child stays dry; a toad landlord evicts them in the rain — years later the child, now an engineer, builds the town's flood defense, buys the building, and hands the now-poor toad an umbrella.
**Cast:** Frog mother (hero) · Toad landlord (villain) · Newt clerk (betrayer) · tadpole (innocent) · Heron mayor (justice).
**MODE:** Standalone 100s. (Mercy ending — variety from pure revenge.)

## 👬 SIBLINGS (anh em / chị em)

### 36. Brothers of the Mine  · 📺 · 🟣 S
**TOPIC:** Two mole brothers dig coal to survive; the elder takes the blame for a collapse so the younger goes free, and a badger mine-owner lets him rot — until the younger becomes a mine inspector, reopens the case, frees his brother, and shuts the owner's deathtrap.
**Cast:** Mole brothers (heroes) · Badger mine-owner (villain) · Rat foreman (betrayer) · younger brother as a kit (innocent, flashback) · Owl magistrate (justice).
**MODE:** 📺 5 parts — *The Collapse / The Sacrifice / The Prison Years / The Inspector / The Reopening*.

### 37. The Smaller Twin  · 🎬/📺 · 🔵 A
**TOPIC:** A peahen favors her dazzling chick and neglects the plain twin; a fox tutor exploits the favored one's vanity to swindle the family — and it's the "plain" twin's quiet skill that saves them all, finally earning his brother's defense.
**Cast:** Plain peachick (hero) · Fox tutor (villain) · the vain twin (betrayer→redeemed) · the plain twin (innocent) · Owl aunt (justice).
**MODE:** Standalone 120s, or 📺 3 parts — *Favoritism / The Swindle / The Save*.

### 38. One Coat Between Us  · 📺 · 🟣 S
**TOPIC:** An orphaned otter brother and sister share one coat and one dream of the sea; a shark orphanage director sells their late father's boat and splits them up for profit — so the brother traces the paperwork, exposes the director's sell-the-orphans scheme, and finds his sister.
**Cast:** Otter siblings (heroes) · Shark director (villain) · Eel clerk (henchman) · the little sister (innocent) · Orca inspector (justice).
**MODE:** 📺 4 parts — *Separated / The Search / The Ledger / Reunion*.

### 39. The Inheritance  · 🎬 · 🔵 A
**TOPIC:** Two squirrel sisters inherit their father's nut orchard; the elder, seduced by a fox investor, schemes to cut out the younger — until the father's hidden recording reveals the true will and the fox's manipulation, and the sisters unite against him.
**Cast:** Younger squirrel (hero) · Fox investor (villain) · elder sister (betrayer→redeemed) · niece (innocent) · Owl notary (justice).
**MODE:** Standalone 120s.

### 40. Carry You Home  · 🎬 · 🟣 S
**TOPIC:** A young deer carries his lame little brother to school on his back every day; a stag PE coach and a rich lion-cub classmate mock them — so at the town race the elder gives up the win to carry his brother across the line, the clip goes viral, the bullies are shamed, and donations fund the brother's braces.
**Cast:** Deer brothers (heroes) · Stag coach (villain) · Lion-cub bully (henchman) · the lame brother (innocent) · Owl teacher (justice).
**MODE:** Standalone 90-120s.

## 🤝 FRIENDSHIP BETRAYAL (tình bạn phản bội)

### 41. Best Man  · 🎬 · 🔵 A
**TOPIC:** A draft horse builds his best friend the fox's dream tavern for free; the fox takes the credit, the tavern, and even the horse's fiancée — but the fox never paid the suppliers (debt in his name), and the horse, who kept every receipt, ends up owning the place.
**Cast:** Horse carpenter (hero) · Fox friend (villain/betrayer) · the fiancée (betrayer) · the horse's niece (innocent) · Bear accountant (justice).
**MODE:** Standalone 120s.

### 42. The Lab Partners  · 🎬/📺 · 🔵 A
**TOPIC:** Two mice invent a water-purifier in a garage; one secretly sells the patent to a rat corporation — but the betrayed mouse had quietly filed it in both his and their late mentor's name, voiding the deal and leaving the traitor with nothing.
**Cast:** Mouse inventor (hero) · Rat CEO (villain) · partner mouse (betrayer) · the mentor's orphan (innocent) · Owl patent judge (justice).
**MODE:** Standalone 120s, or 📺 3 parts.

### 43. The Climb  · 🎬 · 🟣 S
**TOPIC:** Two mountain goats vow to summit together; near the top one cuts the other's rope to claim the record and a sponsor's prize — but the fallen goat survives, his helmet cam filmed everything, and the truth ends the traitor's career and makes the survivor a legend.
**Cast:** Goat climber (hero) · Ram sponsor (villain) · the rope-cutting friend (betrayer) · the hero's kid watching at home (innocent) · Eagle ranger (justice).
**MODE:** Standalone 90-120s. (Visceral betrayal + caught-on-camera karma.)

### 44. The Recipe Club  · 🎬 · 🟢 B+
**TOPIC:** Three raccoon friends run a food blog; the charming one steals the quiet one's signature dish and a TV deal — until decades of the quiet one's grandmother's notes prove the true origin, and the network drops the thief to sign the real chef.
**Cast:** Quiet raccoon (hero) · TV producer (villain) · charming raccoon (betrayer) · grandmother + kit (innocents) · Owl producer (justice).
**MODE:** Standalone 120s.

### 45. The Loan  · 🎬 · 🔵 A
**TOPIC:** A hardworking pig lends his life savings to his best friend the weasel to "save" the weasel's shop; the weasel ghosts him and buys a sports car — then the pig's tiny corner stall blows up after a kindness clip, and he buys the bank foreclosing on the weasel.
**Cast:** Pig vendor (hero) · weasel friend (villain/betrayer) · Crocodile banker (secondary villain) · pig's son (innocent) · community (chorus).
**MODE:** Standalone 90s.

## 🏫 SCHOOL / BULLYING (học đường)

### 46. The Free-Lunch Kid  · 📺/🎬 · 🟣 S
**TOPIC:** A poor hedgehog scholarship student is bullied by a rich lion cub for taking free lunches, and a wolf headmaster sides with the donor's son — until the hedgehog tops the national exam, earns a full ride, and years later becomes the surgeon who saves the bully's father.
**Cast:** Hedgehog student (hero) · Wolf headmaster (villain) · Lion-cub bully (henchman→humbled) · the hedgehog (innocent) · Owl teacher (justice).
**MODE:** 📺 4 parts — *The Lunch / The Humiliation / The Exam / The Surgery*, or Standalone 120s.

### 47. The Wrong Uniform  · 🎬 · 🔵 A
**TOPIC:** A duckling wears a hand-me-down uniform two sizes too big; a swan queen-bee and her clique humiliate her while a peacock principal looks away — until a photo reveals the grandmother sewed it from the principal's late mother's donated cloth, shaming him and turning the school against the bullies.
**Cast:** Duckling (hero/innocent) · Swan queen-bee (villain) · Peacock principal (villain) · grandmother (helper) · Owl counselor (justice).
**MODE:** Standalone 100s.

### 48. Last Pick  · 🎬 · 🔵 A
**TOPIC:** A small penguin is always picked last and benched by a tiger coach who favors the star cub — until the star gets hurt in the final, the benched penguin wins with a move his late father taught him, and the coach is exposed for taking sponsor bribes.
**Cast:** Penguin kid (hero) · Tiger coach (villain) · star cub (henchman) · the penguin (innocent) · Bear referee (justice).
**MODE:** Standalone 120s.

### 49. The Science Fair  · 🎬 · 🔵 A
**TOPIC:** A nearly-blind mole girl builds a brilliant project while a rich fox kid buys a flashy kit and a teacher rigs the judging — until the mole's gas-leak detector actually saves the school during the fair, exposing the rigged contest.
**Cast:** Mole student (hero) · Stoat teacher (villain) · Fox kid (betrayer) · the mole (innocent) · Owl judge (justice).
**MODE:** Standalone 90-120s.

### 50. The Scholarship  · 🎬 · 🟢 B+
**TOPIC:** A poor squirrel works three jobs for a scholarship that a wealthy raccoon family bribes away for their lazy son — until her essay about her late mother goes viral, a foundation funds her directly, and the bribery is exposed.
**Cast:** Squirrel student (hero) · Raccoon donor (villain) · Dean (betrayer) · the squirrel (innocent) · Owl trustee (justice).
**MODE:** Standalone 100s.

## 👴 ELDERLY & LEGACY (người già / di sản)

### 51. Grandfather's Watch  · 📺 · 🔵 A
**TOPIC:** An elderly tortoise watchmaker is pushed into a home by a vulture "care" corporation that seizes his shop, but his grandkit refuses to abandon him — and the old grandfather clock hides war medals and a deed proving the corporation built on his stolen land.
**Cast:** Tortoise grandfather + grandkit (heroes) · Vulture care-corp boss (villain) · Hyena administrator (betrayer) · grandkit (innocent) · Owl judge (justice).
**MODE:** 📺 3 parts — *The Home / The Clock / The Deed*.

### 52. The Park Bench  · 🎬 · 🔵 A
**TOPIC:** An old donkey feeds pigeons on the same bench for forty years until a rhino developer rips out the park and has him removed — but the bench is a memorial he carved for his late wife, the town's founder, protected by a charter he kept, and the tower is halted.
**Cast:** Donkey elder (hero) · Rhino developer (villain) · Crow official (betrayer) · a child who befriends him (innocent) · Elephant mayor (justice).
**MODE:** Standalone 120s.

### 53. The Last Harvest  · 🎬/📺 · 🔵 A
**TOPIC:** An aging ox is told by his fox-banker nephew that the farm is worthless and is tricked into signing it away — but it sits on the route of a new railway worth a fortune (the nephew knew), and the ox's granddaughter finds the survey that voids the sale.
**Cast:** Ox grandfather + granddaughter (heroes) · Fox nephew banker (villain/betrayer) · Snake lawyer (henchman) · granddaughter (innocent) · Owl judge (justice).
**MODE:** Standalone 120s, or 📺 3 parts.

### 54. Tea for One  · 🎬 · 🟢 B+
**TOPIC:** A widowed badger runs a tiny tea house nobody visits; a cat café-chain mogul mocks it and opens next door to crush him — until a post about the lonely badger fills his shop and the chain, built on unpaid staff, collapses.
**Cast:** Badger (hero) · Cat chain mogul (villain) · Rat manager (betrayer) · a lonely kit who visits (innocent) · community (chorus).
**MODE:** Standalone 90s.

## 💔 LOVE / MARRIAGE BETRAYAL & REDEMPTION

### 55. The Architect's Wife  · 📺 · 🟣 S
**TOPIC:** A swan leaves her loyal crane husband and their cygnet for a wealthy flamingo, calling the crane a failure; the crane raises the cygnet alone — and rises to become the celebrated architect who designed the very city she ends up begging to live in, while the flamingo's fortune is exposed as fraud.
**Cast:** Crane father (hero) · Flamingo tycoon (villain) · Swan wife (betrayer) · cygnet (innocent) · Owl judge (justice).
**MODE:** 📺 5 parts — *The Departure / Raising the Cygnet / The Rise / The Fraud / The Reckoning*. (Closest to the proven "Dog Swings Alone" engine.)

### 56. Second Chance  · 🎬/📺 · 🔵 A
**TOPIC:** A lioness abandons her cub for a rich tiger; years later, broke and sick, she returns to find the cub raised well by the gentle ox she once mocked — and the cub chooses the ox as his real father but quietly pays for the dying lioness's care.
**Cast:** Ox stepfather + cub (heroes) · Tiger (villain) · Lioness (betrayer→repentant) · cub (innocent) · Owl elder (justice).
**MODE:** Standalone 120s, or 📺 4 parts. (Mercy ending, deep emotion.)

### 57. The Ring He Kept  · 🎬 · 🔵 A
**TOPIC:** A poor mouse is dumped by his cat fiancée for a rich rat the night before the wedding, and he keeps the ring — years later the cat, ruined by the rat, returns, but the now-successful mouse gives the ring to the kind hedgehog who loved him all along.
**Cast:** Mouse (hero) · Rat tycoon (villain) · Cat (betrayer) · — · Hedgehog (true love).
**MODE:** Standalone 90-120s.

### 58. For the Pups  · 🎬 · 🔵 A
**TOPIC:** A fox single dad fights his wealthy wolf ex and her snake lawyer for custody in a court biased toward money — until the kits testify that their dad reads to them every night, and a hidden recording exposes the ex's neglect, winning him custody.
**Cast:** Fox father (hero) · Wolf ex + Snake lawyer (villains) · the kits (innocents) · Owl judge (justice).
**MODE:** Standalone 120s.

## 🧠 HIGH-CONCEPT

### 59. The Substitute  · 🎬 · 🔵 A
**TOPIC:** A janitor mouse secretly solves the equations a famous lion professor takes credit for; exposed and fired as "just the help," he's mocked — until a visiting owl academy recognizes his handwriting on a breakthrough and offers him the chair the lion wanted.
**Cast:** Mouse janitor (hero) · Lion professor (villain) · Dean (betrayer) · mouse's daughter (innocent) · Owl academy head (justice).
**MODE:** Standalone 120s. (Underdog-genius energy.)

### 60. The Whole Village  · 📺 · 🟣 S
**TOPIC:** A crocodile mega-developer buys an animal village to flatten it for a resort, evicting a beaver, an ox, a hen, and an owl one by one — until the smallest, a single field-mouse mother, discovers the dam plan will flood the valley, and each "worthless" little one the croc bullied holds one piece of the evidence that sinks him.
**Cast:** Field-mouse mother (hero) · Crocodile developer (villain) · Heron official (betrayer) · the village kits (innocents) · Owl judge (justice) — ensemble.
**MODE:** 📺 6 parts — one neighbor evicted per part, then *The Discovery* + *The Reckoning*. (Ensemble finale that can crossover with your other series into one universe.)
