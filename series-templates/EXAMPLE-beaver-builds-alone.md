## 1. Logline
A hardworking river **beaver** carpenter, mocked and abandoned, is stripped of the
**boathouse** he built by an arrogant **lion** developer — until the lion's own
greed sinks him and the beaver reclaims the riverbank lot, which hides a fortune.

## 2. Title system
- **Series title:** "Beaver Builds Alone"
- **Caption hook:** "He built it with his own paws. Then they took the river from him."

## 4. Through-line
- **Promise object:** a hand-carved **little wooden boat** Buck is building for Pip. States: being carved (P1) -> mocked/seized (P2) -> smashed during demolition (P3) -> rebuilt, finished, sailed together (P7).
- **Catchphrase:** **"Almost there, kiddo."** — said while carving (P1, P3), fulfilled on the water (P7).
- **Insult to reverse:** "You're driftwood, Buck. You always were." (P1) -> reversed silently when Buck pulls up in a yacht (P6).
- **Hidden payload (poetic justice):** the muddy riverbank Lot 12 hides an **old sunken bank-safe full of gold** under the silt — Crane bulldozes it for a resort, never digs; Buck, a digger by nature, finds it. Planted P2 (Crane brags the lot is "worthless mud"), detonated P7.

| 7 | The Boat Sails | ULTIMATE REVENGE | Reunion + boat finished; Crane learns "some beaver named Buck" owns the lot | Crane, in prison, whispers "Buck…" |

# CONDENSED SCRIPTS (proof of execution)

### PART 1 — THE COLD DEPARTURE  (~60s, ~125 words) — beat: LOSS
- **HOOK (0-2s):** CU on Vera's beak curling in disgust. "You're driftwood, Buck. You always were."
- **Scene 1 — the dock at dusk.** Buck whittles the little boat; Pip watches.
  - Pip: "Dad, when's the boat gonna float?"
  - Buck: "Almost there, kiddo."
  - Vera: "I can't live in a pile of sticks anymore. Look at this place."
  - Buck: "I'm building us a home. For Pip. Give me time."
  - Vera: "Driftwood. That's all you'll ever be."
- **Scene 2 — the lion's car (cross-cut).** Crane waits by a black car; Vera steps in. Villa pool; Crane swirls wine.
  - Vera: "I almost wasted my life in that mudhole, Crane."
  - Crane: "Don't think about the little guy. He got washed up."
  - Buck (on the dock, alone): "Almost there, kiddo. Almost there."
- **CLIFFHANGER:** Pip in the back window: "I'll come back to sail it, dad. I promise." Buck waving, eyes wet: "I'll be right here, son."

### PART 2 — THE RIVERBANK GRAB  (~62s, ~135 words) — beat: INJUSTICE
- **HOOK:** Crow clerk slides a paper across a desk. Crane: "Buy the lien. All of it."
- **Scene 1 — night reunion.** Pip sneaks from the villa to Buck's shack; they hug. "I promised I'd come back."
- **Scene 2 — the scheme (cross-cut).** Vera finds Pip's bed empty; Crane phones Razz.
  - Crane: "The lot, the dock, the cute little boat — everything. That mud's worthless anyway, but the *view* sells."
- **Scene 3 — the notice.** Crow (reluctant) + Razz at Buck's gate.
  - Crow: "Mr. Buck… the lien on your land was purchased. You have 7 days."
  - Buck: "This is my riverbank! I built this dock with my own paws!"
  - Crane: "You built it on *my* river now, little guy."
- **CLIFFHANGER:** Pip clings to the dock post, crying: "Dad, what's happening?!"

### PART 3 — TEAR DOWN THE DOCK  (~60s, ~115 words) — beat: ENDURANCE
- **HOOK:** A foreman tosses Buck a crowbar. "You the demo guy? Whole dock comes down today."
- **Scene 1:** Buck realizes it's *his* boathouse. "I built this place."
- **Scene 2 (cross-cut):** Pip watches from the resort window as his dad does the labor.
  - Crane: "Orange vest suits you, Buck."
  - Vera: "Sit down, Pip. That worker's nobody."
- **Scene 3 — the boat smashed.** Crane makes Buck toss the little carved boat in the skip to clear the site.
  - Crane: "You're better at breaking things than building them."
  - Pip (clutching the family photo, eyes hardening): "You took my dad's river. I'm gonna take everything you have, Crane."
- **CLIFFHANGER:** hold on Pip's cold stare.

### PART 4 — THE VAULT BY THE RIVER  (~70s, ~150 words) — beat: AWAKENING
- **HOOK:** Pip cracks a door; Crane & Razz hunch over a blueprint. "Midnight. The vault opens at 12:15. 90 seconds."
- **Scene 1:** Razz: "Clean in, clean out. No traces."
- **Scene 2 — the tip-off.** Pip under the blanket, phone glow on his face.
  - Pip: "There's gonna be a robbery at Riverbend Bank. Tonight. Midnight. A lion and a hyena. Please hurry."
- **Scene 3 (cross-cut):** at the vault Crane stuffs cash; sirens; he bolts in the rain, dumps the bag.
  - Crane: "Easiest job we ever pulled, Razz."
- **CLIFFHANGER:** soaked Crane bursts home, grabs Vera: "Someone tipped them off! I'm gonna find out who." Pip, on the floor with the photo, half-smiles: "I'm gonna find out who did this."

### PART 5 — CAUGHT IN THE CURRENT  (~70s, ~150 words) — beat: KARMA
- **HOOK:** Doorbell. Owl on the step. "Mr. Crane. Detective Hollow. We found a bag by Riverbend. Your prints are all over it."
- **Scene 1:** Crane: "No idea what you mean." Hollow: "Cooperate, or we come back with a warrant."
- **Scene 2 — Vera bails again.** She packs jewels; Pip on the stairs.
  - Pip: "You're leaving again, aren't you, mom?"
  - Vera: "Move, Pip. Your father's driftwood. Crane's a crook. And you're in my way."
- **Scene 3 — testimony.** Pip to Hollow: "I made the call. I heard him plan it — him and a hyena in a black coat. He stole my dad's river. He made my dad smash my boat."
- **CLIFFHANGER:** Crane cuffed, roaring: "Do you know who I am? I own this river!" Pip, alone, to the photo: "I did it, dad."

### PART 6 — THE GOLDEN SILT  (~65s, ~45 words) — beat: REBIRTH
- **HOOK:** Buck in his old truck checks a balance: $11,240. "Riverbend Lot 12. Cash only. I'm standing on it. I want it."
- **Scene 1 — the dig.** Buck shovels the "worthless mud"; the blade clangs on metal — a half-sunk old safe, split open, packed with gold bars.
  - Buck: "Almost there, kiddo." / (touching gold) "I can't believe my eyes."
- **Scene 2 — the glow-up.** Gold to Owl-run bank; new suit; mirror: "Still me. Just cleaner." Buys a small yacht.
- **CLIFFHANGER:** Buck idles the yacht by the dock; across the water, Crane is perp-walked. Their eyes meet. Buck calmly motors off.

### PART 7 — THE BOAT SAILS  (~72s, ~155 words) — beat: ULTIMATE REVENGE
- **HOOK:** Sunset dock. "Dad!" Pip runs; they embrace.
  - Buck: "I'm here, son. I told you I'm not going anywhere."
- **Scene 1 — promise kept.** A finished, gleaming wooden boat (rebuilt) rocks on the water.
  - Pip: "You finished it." / Buck: "Almost there, kiddo. I always meant it." They sail.
- **Scene 2 — prison (cross-cut).** Crane in prison blues; Razz visiting.
  - Crane: "Frozen accounts. They sold everything."
  - Razz: "Even the river lot. Lot 12. Some buyer, cash, same day."
- **Scene 3 — the twist.** Razz: "There were millions in gold under that silt. My way back." Crane: "WHO bought it?!" Razz: "Some beaver. Said his name was Buck."
- **CLIFFHANGER / BUTTON:** Crane shuts his eyes, jaw grinding: "Buck…" Cut to Buck & Pip laughing on the boat, golden light.

## How this was generated (so you can repeat it)
1. Picked a **HERO with a craft** (beaver = builder) from the casting table.
2. Picked a **TYRANT predator** who can plausibly ruin that craft (lion developer takes the riverbank).
3. Chose a **BETRAYER close to the hero** (peacock wife) and an **INNOCENT** (kit).
4. Chose **JUSTICE** (owl) and a **HENCHMAN who leaks the secret** (hyena).
5. Invented a **promise object + catchphrase** (carved boat / "Almost there, kiddo").
6. Planted a **poetic-justice payload** (gold under the "worthless mud").
7. Mapped all of it onto the **7-beat arc** and wrote each part with the per-part template.
